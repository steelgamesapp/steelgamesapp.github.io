**Name:** SteelGames Price Check

**Summary (132 chars max):** See the best price and lowest price ever on Steam game pages, plus veteran and first responder discounts.

**Category:** Shopping

**Description:**
SteelGames Price Check adds a small box to every Steam game page that shows:
- The best current price across official stores and authorized key sellers
- The lowest price ever recorded, and whether today's price matches it
- One-click links to compare every store and see the price history
- Discounts for veterans, military members, police, firefighters and EMTs

Built by SteelGames, a veteran-owned PC game deals site. No account, no ads, no tracking. Prices come from the CheapShark service.

**Single purpose:** Show price comparison information on Steam store game pages.

**Permission justifications**
- storage: caches recent price results for a few hours so pages load faster.
- Host access to www.cheapshark.com: fetches game prices from the CheapShark API.
- Content script on store.steampowered.com/app/*: reads the game number from the page address and shows the price box.
