Questions

Why do the buttons open a search instead of one product?

The same product is often not sold in every country, or has a different listing there. A search on your local store always shows what you can actually buy and have delivered.

Do I pay more because of these links?

No. The price is whatever Amazon charges in your country. See the note at the bottom of the page about commissions.

Where does my country come from?

Your browser asks a free country lookup run by Cloudflare, and we keep the answer in your browser for a week. If that fails, we use your browser language. We do not store it on our servers. You can always change it above.
