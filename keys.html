Buying keys safely

  • Prefer authorized retailers. Stores labeled authorized work directly with publishers, so keys are far less likely to be revoked.
  • Treat marketplaces with care. On a marketplace, individual sellers list the keys. Prices can be low, but quality depends on the seller, so check seller ratings and the refund terms.
  • Check the region. Some keys only activate in certain countries. Read the product page before you pay.
  • Check the platform. Make sure the key is for Steam, Epic, GOG or the launcher you actually use.
  • Redeem quickly. Redeem a key soon after purchase so any problem is covered by the store's refund window.
  • Pay with protection. A card or PayPal gives you a dispute route if a key fails.
  • Compare to the official price. If a key is far below its lowest price ever, be skeptical.
