About ScourgeBringer

ScourgeBringer is a fast roguelite action platformer. You play Kyhra, a warrior exploring a hostile, unknown place filled with ancient machines that guard a seal tied to her past and possibly to humanity's future. Rooms lock until you clear them, and you can dash and float freely through the air while slicing through enemies.

Each run builds a new layout, and you unlock lasting upgrades as you go. It is a single-player game with sharp pixel art and very quick combat, best suited to players who enjoy demanding roguelites and fluid movement, and who are happy to die often while improving.
