About Guacamelee! Gold Edition

Wrestling moves double as both weapons and keys in Guacamelee! Gold Edition, an exploration-driven platformer in the Metroidvania mold, set in a world drawn from Mexican culture and folklore. The hero is a luchador, and moves such as the Rooster Uppercut serve in combat and also for reaching new places, so fighting and platforming feed into each other. A dimension-swap mechanic shifts the world between two states, revealing hidden areas that are otherwise unreachable.

A second player can join in local co-op, and the Gold Edition is a fuller release of the game. It suits fans of Metroidvanias and melee brawlers who enjoy colorful, folklore-inspired settings and are happy to backtrack as new abilities unlock.
