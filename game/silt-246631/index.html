About SILT

Silt is an atmospheric puzzle adventure set in a surreal ocean abyss. You play a lone diver chained in the deep, who can possess sea creatures and use their abilities to solve environmental puzzles.

The black-and-white art creates a haunting mood, and the mystery of what lies below unfolds without words. Some creatures can break walls, others can move fast or light the way. It is a short single-player game for players who enjoy dark, artistic puzzles.
