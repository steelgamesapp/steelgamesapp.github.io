About WRC 9 FIA World Rally Championship

Kenya, Japan and New Zealand join the calendar in this official FIA World Rally Championship simulation, along with 35 new special stages and more than 15 classic cars. Drivers and cars from three WRC categories are included, and you tackle stages against the clock using updated physics, rebuilt engine audio and redesigned pacenotes that guide you through each corner.

A Clubs system lets players create their own championships and invite others to compete online, and custom championships are available to build as well. WRC 9 is aimed at rally enthusiasts who want a simulation-style driving challenge, and it supports single-player and online play, with full controller support.
