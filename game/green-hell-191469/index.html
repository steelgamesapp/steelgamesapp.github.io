About Green Hell

Dropped into the Amazon rainforest with almost nothing, you have to stay alive. You craft tools, hunt and forage for food, build a shelter, and look after both your body and your mind, inspecting yourself for injuries and keeping your sanity from slipping. The campaign follows anthropologist Jake Higgins as he looks for Mia, his missing wife, and runs for more than 25 hours.

The survival systems lean on real-world techniques, and the jungle is unforgiving. You can face it alone or bring friends along in online co-op, which shifts the mood from isolated dread to shared problem-solving. It suits players who like slow, demanding survival games with a story attached.
