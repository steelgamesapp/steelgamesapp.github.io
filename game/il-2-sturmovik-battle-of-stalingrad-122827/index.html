About IL-2 Sturmovik: Battle of Stalingrad

Flying a World War II aircraft over Stalingrad is the heart of this combat flight simulation, the latest in the long-running IL-2 Sturmovik series. A single-player Career mode puts you in the role of a combat pilot and can be played across the Stalingrad, Moscow and Kuban theatres you own.

The simulation aims for more realistic aerodynamics and physics than earlier entries, at a larger scale. Co-op and online PvP modes are included, and VR is supported. It suits dedicated flight sim fans who want an accurate recreation of wartime air battles.
