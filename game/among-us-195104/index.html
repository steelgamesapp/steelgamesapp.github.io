About Among Us

Among Us is an online and local party game of teamwork and betrayal for 4 to 15 players, set on a spaceship. Most players are Crewmates trying to finish tasks around the map, while one or more are secret Impostors trying to sabotage the ship and pick everyone off.

When a body is found or an emergency meeting is called, everyone debates who looks suspicious and votes someone out. Bluffing, alibis and quick accusations make every round different, especially with friends on voice chat. Matches are short, the rules are easy to learn and it runs well on modest PCs.
