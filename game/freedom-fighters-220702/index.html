About Freedom Fighters

Freedom Fighters is a third-person shooter set in a version of history where the Soviet Union has invaded the United States. In occupied New York, you play Chris Stone, a plumber who joins the resistance after his brother is taken. As the campaign goes on, Chris earns the trust of more fighters, and you can recruit them into a squad and give them simple orders to attack, defend or follow.

Originally released in 2003 by IO Interactive, the game is single-player and moves at a brisk pace, mixing street fights with sabotage objectives across the city. Freedom Fighters suits players looking for a classic squad-based action game with straightforward controls and a short, focused campaign.
