About Sumire

Sumire, a girl who has lost her spark, crosses paths with a playful spirit in a Japanese village and strikes a deal: finish a list of tasks before nightfall and her biggest wish comes true. The requests come from woodland creatures, villagers and her own needs, and you choose which ones to accept.

Time is limited, and opportunities fade as the day moves on, so a request you refuse may never return. Collectibles, bonus items and skill-based challenges are scattered about, some of them hidden. The mood is magical and relaxed, and it suits players who enjoy narrative adventures with branching quests. Single-player, with full controller support.
