About Ad Infinitum

Memories of the First World War haunt a German soldier in this game of psychological horror. You alternate between two settings: a run-down, deserted manor that was your childhood home, where you wake in your teenage bedroom, and the trenches of the front line, where nightmarish creatures lurk among the horrors of war. As dream and reality blur, you work to reconstruct your past and break a cycle of suffering.

Fear and confusion drive the mood of Ad Infinitum, set against a war backdrop. It is a single-player game with full controller support, suited to horror fans interested in a historical setting and a story about trauma.
