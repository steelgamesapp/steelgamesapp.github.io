About METRO Last Light Redux

Metro: Last Light Redux is a first-person shooter set in 2034, beneath the ruins of post-apocalyptic Moscow. You play Artyom, a Ranger trying to protect the remnants of humanity living in the Metro tunnels from mutants and rival factions.

The game mixes stealth, survival and horror, with scarce ammo, gas masks and a tense atmosphere. This Redux version improves the visuals and includes all extra content, plus the challenging Ranger mode. It is a single-player game for fans of story-driven shooters.
