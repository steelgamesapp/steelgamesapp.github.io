About Fire: Ungh’s Quest

Ungh, a lovably chaotic Neanderthal, dozes off during his first night watch, and the village fire goes out. Banished for it, he must find a new flame, and you help him search the Stone Age world, from the inside of a dinosaur to volcanoes and even through time itself.

This puzzle-adventure responds to a single click or swipe, with intuitive puzzles that need only a little logic. The story is told with few words and a savagely funny streak. It is a single-player game from Daedalic Entertainment that suits casual adventure fans who like light puzzling and offbeat humor.
