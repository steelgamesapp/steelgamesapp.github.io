About The Next BIG Thing

Hollywood gets a monstrous makeover in this comic adventure from Pendulo Studios. The creatures in horror films turn out to be real monsters, and some are now stuck making children's movies, romantic comedies and musicals. Unsurprisingly, they begin to rebel. You follow journalist Liz Allaire and Dan Murray as they get drawn into the chaos, solving puzzles across settings modeled on different film genres.

The studio behind the Runaway series built this one around jokes, movie tributes and a few mysteries. Its presentation aims for the look of an animated feature, with a full soundtrack and plenty of dialogue. It is single-player and suits adventure fans who enjoy silly puzzles and affectionate film parody.
