About State of Mind

Berlin, 2048: resources are scarce, the air and water are polluted, and drones and humanoid robots fill public jobs while surveillance is everywhere. Richard Nolan, a journalist who speaks out against this trend, wakes in a hospital after an explosion to find his wife and son missing. His search draws him into a conflict between a grim physical world and the promise of a digital paradise.

This futuristic thriller from Daedalic Entertainment explores transhumanism and themes of separation and reunification. It is a single-player, story-driven adventure with full controller support, suited to players who enjoy serious science fiction and character drama over action.
