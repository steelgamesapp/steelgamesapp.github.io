About Rising Storm 2: Vietnam

Rising Storm 2: Vietnam is a multiplayer shooter set during the Vietnam War, part of the Red Orchestra series from Tripwire Interactive and Antimatter Games. Matches hold up to 64 players and pit American and allied forces against North Vietnamese and Viet Cong troops across jungle, village and city maps. Each side fights differently, with helicopters on one side and traps and tunnels on the other.

There is no story campaign; this is an online team game with deadly weapons, where a single hit often ends a life. It suits players who like realistic, team-focused shooters and are happy to communicate with a squad.
