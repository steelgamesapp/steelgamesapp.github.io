About AER Memories of Old

You play one of the last shapeshifters, sent on a pilgrimage across a world that has been broken into islands floating in the sky. The journey leads through ancient ruins and temples full of puzzles and mysteries, with the goal of finding what will stop reality from falling into darkness. You can shift into bird form whenever you like to fly between the islands.

The open world is built around discovery, with hidden secrets to find, and the developers put a strong emphasis on mood and visual style. The art is minimalist but vibrant. It is a single-player game with full controller support that suits players who prefer quiet exploration and atmosphere.
