About Deceive Inc.

Deceive Inc. is a multiplayer game about rival spies competing for the same prize. Each match drops several agents into a location full of ordinary people, and you can disguise yourself as anyone there to move around unnoticed. The goal is to reach the objective and get it out, while other players try to spot you and do the same.

You choose from a cast of agents with different gadgets and abilities, and matches mix social stealth with sudden firefights. You can play alone or in a small team with friends online, but there is no single-player mode. It suits players who like tense, competitive sessions built on bluffing and reading other players.
