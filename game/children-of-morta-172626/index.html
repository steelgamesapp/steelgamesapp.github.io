About Children of Morta

The Bergsons are a family of heroes, and you lead them against the Corruption by fighting through dungeons and caves that are generated fresh on each run. Rather than a single character, you switch between family members, each with their own abilities, in a mix of action RPG and rogue-lite design with hack-and-slash combat.

Leveling up develops both individual heroes and the family as a whole. Online and local co-op are available, including the Story mode and Family Trials, and full controller support is included. It suits players who like story-driven action RPGs with rogue-lite runs and a focus on family.
