About A New Beginning - Final Cut

Climate catastrophe is closing in on Earth in this point-and-click adventure, presented in the style of a graphic novel. Bioengineer Bent Svensson, who left energy research to live quietly in the forests of Norway, gets an unexpected visitor: a woman named Fay, who says she has come back from the future. She insists his work on alternative energy is humanity's last hope, and the two must travel the world to see it through.

The tone balances a serious environmental premise with wit. This Final Cut edition from Daedalic Entertainment is single-player and suits adventure fans who prefer story and dialogue to action.
