About The Forest Quartet

Grief, music and saying goodbye sit at the center of The Forest Quartet. You play as the spirit of a lead singer who has died, and you return to visit the three members of her old band. Each gets an act of their own, set in a world shaped by their feelings, and the goal is helping them face their loss so the band can perform one last concert together.

It is a single-player game that mixes environmental puzzles with some light action against a dark corruption tied to each character. The pace is steady and reflective, and the story carries much of the weight. It suits players looking for a short, emotional indie adventure with a strong musical theme.
