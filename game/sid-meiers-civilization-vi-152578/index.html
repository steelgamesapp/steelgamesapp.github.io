About Sid Meier’s Civilization® VI

Lead a civilization from its first settlement toward one of five kinds of victory in this turn-based strategy game. Cities spread across the map, with districts, wonders and improvements each placed on separate hexes, so you choose which specializations to develop.

Research into technology and culture opens up new possibilities, and rival leaders pursue agendas shaped by their historical traits, so each opponent plays a little differently. You can play solo or against other players online. It suits strategy fans who enjoy long campaigns and shaping an empire's layout turn by turn.
