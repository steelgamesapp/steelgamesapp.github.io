About Tengami

Tengami is a gentle adventure whose world is a pop-up book drawn from Japanese art and folklore. Everything is made of paper, and you move through it by turning pages, pulling tabs and folding scenes open or closed, which changes the landscape and reveals new paths. Puzzles are built into these paper mechanics, and the setting draws on traditional Japanese art, with shrines, forests and changing seasons.

It is a short single-player game with a calm pace and no combat, meant to be taken slowly and enjoyed for its look and sound as much as its puzzles. Tengami suits players who like quiet, artistic games and puzzles that reward observation rather than quick reactions.
