About Filament

Filament is a puzzle game set aboard a ship called The Alabaster, whose crew has gone missing. You guide a small craft that trails a cable behind it, and each puzzle asks you to wrap that cable around a set of pylons in the right order to light them up without tangling or blocking your own path. As you work through the ship, you learn more about what became of the people who were on board.

It is a single-player game with a calm pace and hundreds of puzzles that grow steadily harder. A hint system is available for the toughest ones. Filament is a good pick for players who enjoy logic puzzles and a quiet mystery in the background.
