About Death's Door

Deaths Door is an action adventure in which you play a crow whose job is to collect the souls of the dead. When a soul assigned to you is stolen, you have to chase the thief into a strange land where nothing dies and its creatures have lived far too long. The world is connected through a hub of doors, and the story has a dry, quiet humor.

It is viewed from an isometric angle and combines sword combat, ranged attacks, puzzles and challenging boss fights, in the style of classic Zelda games. Deaths Door is a single-player game with a moderate length, and it suits players who like tight combat and exploring a moody, handcrafted world.
