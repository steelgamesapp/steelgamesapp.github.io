About Fell Seal: Arbiter's Mark

Kyrie is an Arbiter, a servant of the Immortal Council, which exists to preserve order across a fantasy land touched by steampunk. You lead her group of Arbiters through more than 40 story encounters in a mature narrative, with demanding end-game content waiting afterward. Battles are classic turn-based tactics on grid maps with rough terrain and elevation, set on hand-drawn backdrops.

Character building stands out. Each character's main class, secondary class and passives come from a roster of 30-plus classes and 300-plus abilities, so you can shape a flexible all-rounder or a narrow specialist. Single-player, from 6 Eyes Studio, it suits tactics fans who enjoy building their party.
