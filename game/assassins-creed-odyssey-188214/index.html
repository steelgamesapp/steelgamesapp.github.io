About Assassin's Creed® Odyssey

Set in Ancient Greece during the struggle between Sparta and Athens, Assassin's Creed Odyssey is an open-world action RPG in which you play a Spartan warrior. You roam islands and cities, fight on land and at sea, and rise from outcast to legendary figure while searching for the truth about your past.

Decisions carry weight here: alliances, conflicts and the fates of certain characters shift according to what you choose. You can tailor your gear, your ship and your special abilities to suit how you like to fight, and the large clashes between the two city-states can involve 150 soldiers per side. It is a single-player game that suits players who like big historical settings with room to roam.
