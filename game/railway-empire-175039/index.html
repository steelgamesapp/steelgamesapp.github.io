About Railway Empire

Starting in 1830, you head a railroad company in North America as industry booms and rivals race to build the dominant rail network. You lay track, buy trains from a catalog of more than 40 detailed models, construct stations, factories, maintenance facilities and tourist sites, and hire staff to keep services efficient. Over 300 technologies can be researched to improve both the trains and the way the company operates.

Competitors pursue the same goals, so the game combines logistics, finance and long-term planning rather than simple track-laying. It is single-player with controller support, and it suits players who enjoy building transport networks and tuning them for profit across a long span of history.
