About The Smurfs – Dreams

The Smurfs - Dreams is a 3D platformer where the fate of the Smurf Village depends on what happens in your dreams. You dive into a series of magical dream worlds to save the village from a spell that has put the Smurfs to sleep.

Each dream world has its own theme and challenges, with platforming, puzzles and abilities to unlock. The colorful style suits young players, and you can team up with a friend in local co-op. It is a fun pick for families and Smurfs fans.
