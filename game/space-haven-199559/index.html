About Space Haven

Space Haven is a spaceship colony sim from Bugbyte. You lead a ragtag crew of civilians searching for a new home among the stars, building spaceships tile by tile as you go.

You manage oxygen and gas levels, food, power and the moods and relationships of your crew. Along the way you meet other space-faring groups to trade or fight with, board derelicts and explore the galaxy. It suits players who enjoy deep management games in the style of RimWorld with a sci-fi twist.
