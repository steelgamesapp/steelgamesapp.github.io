About Lacuna – A Sci-Fi Noir Adventure

You play Neil Conrad, an agent with the CDI, who is drawn into a murder investigation that could lead the solar system to war. You question people, collect evidence and decide how to act on what you learn, in an adventure driven mostly by conversation and set in a sci-fi noir future.

Choices branch the story toward different endings, and decisions are final once made. The 2D art leans into a noir atmosphere. It suits players who enjoy reading, weighing hard calls and replaying for other outcomes more than action. It is single-player with full controller support.
