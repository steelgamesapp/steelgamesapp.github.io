About We Were Here Expeditions: The FriendShip

We Were Here Expeditions: The FriendShip is a short, standalone entry in the We Were Here series of co-op puzzle games. Two players set out on a voyage together, each seeing different parts of a puzzle, and the only way through is to describe what you see and work out the solution together. The tone is lighter than earlier entries, built around a friendship-testing excursion.

It is strictly a two-player online co-op game with no solo mode, and communication by voice chat is essential. Sessions are brief, so it can be finished in an evening, and puzzles change between rounds for replay. It suits pairs of friends or partners who enjoy escape-room style puzzles and talking things through.
