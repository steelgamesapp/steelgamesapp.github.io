About Overpass

Overpass is a rhythm game played from behind the wheel. You drive along a futuristic road through a stylized city, and the track, the skyline and the obstacles all move in time with the music. Steering, drifting and hitting your timing on the beat are what carry you forward on this musical road trip.

It is a single-player game from the small independent Studio Bean, focused on mood and flow more than on racing against rivals. It suits players who like rhythm games and calm driving, and who enjoy music-led experiences with a strong visual style that can be played in short sessions.
