About Gibbous - A Cthulhu Adventure

Darkham is a town with a murky past where odd cults keep appearing, and this comedy cosmic horror point-and-click game unfolds there. Don R. Ketype, a detective, is hunting the Necronomicon, but librarian Buzz Kerwan finds it first and accidentally changes Kitteh, his cat, into a creature that walks and talks. You switch between three protagonists as the story travels across the world, solving puzzles and unraveling old conspiracies.

The look is distinctive: hand-painted HD art with traditional animation, made by a studio in Transylvania. The writing leans on jokes and Lovecraft-inspired touches rather than scares. It is single-player and suits fans of classic adventure games who like a lengthy story and humor-driven puzzles.
