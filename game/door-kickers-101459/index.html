About Door Kickers

Door Kickers is a tactical strategy game in which you command a SWAT team during police operations. Viewed from above, each mission shows a building with suspects and sometimes hostages, and you draw paths for your officers, set when they wait and which doors they breach. Then you watch the plan unfold and can pause to adjust it as things go wrong.

Missions are short and can be replayed to find cleaner solutions, and officers gain experience and better gear over time. It can be played alone or in online co-op. Door Kickers suits players who like planning and careful tactics more than reflex shooting, and who enjoy perfecting a plan.
