About Faraday Protocol

Raug Zeekon, an interstellar archaeologist, is sent by his company to investigate a mysterious signal from an unexplored star in this first-person puzzle game. On arrival he finds the Bia-Tool, an alien relic shaped like a pistol that can absorb energy and send it to other machinery. Using it, you work through temples and solve puzzles that get tougher the deeper you go.

Progress comes from routing energy between devices, which rewards observation and logic. Single-player with full controller support, Faraday Protocol suits players who enjoy methodical, thought-heavy puzzle games and discovering how an unfamiliar tool works.
