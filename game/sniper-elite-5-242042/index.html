About Sniper Elite 5

Brittany, 1944: Karl Fairburne, an American marksman, joins a covert Rangers mission to weaken German coastal fortifications, teams up with the French Resistance and uncovers a secret project known as Operation Kraken. This third-person tactical shooter spreads its missions across large maps modeled on real locations, and you can approach objectives with long-range shots, stealth or direct fighting.

The series' trademark kill camera follows bullets in slow motion as they hit their targets. Beyond the single-player campaign, you can team up online or face other players in PvP modes. Sniper Elite 5 suits people who enjoy patient, methodical shooters with a World War II backdrop.
