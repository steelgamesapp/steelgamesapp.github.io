About Lysfanga: The Time Shift Warrior

Time rewinding is the core trick of Lysfanga: The Time Shift Warrior, a tactical action game starring Ime, a protector of the kingdom of Antala who has been granted temporal powers by the Goddess of Time. Rewinding during combat summons clones of her earlier selves to fight beside her, turning one warrior into a small army against the Raxes, demons that have escaped from ancient cities.

Moving through those cities unlocks additional weapons and spells for facing a larger threat. Planning how and when to use the clones matters as much as quick reactions. It is a single-player game with full controller support, suited to players who enjoy action games with a tactical, puzzle-like layer.
