About Greak: Memories of Azur

Greak: Memories of Azur, a hand-drawn puzzle platformer, follows Greak, Adara and Raydel, three siblings who must escape their homeland of Azur as an invading force and its creatures spread across the land. Each sibling has different skills: one is nimble, one can float and use magic, and one is strong and carries a shield. You switch between them or move them together to solve puzzles and fight.

It is single-player only, and controlling all three at once can be demanding during combat and platforming. The pace is moderate, with exploration and some backtracking. Greak suits players who like adventure games with a storybook look and are comfortable managing several characters at once.
