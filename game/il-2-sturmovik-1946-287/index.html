About IL-2 Sturmovik: 1946

World War II flight simulation fills this collection, which combines the earlier games in the series, among them Forgotten Battles and Pacific Fighters, with the Ace Expansion Pack and campaigns set in an alternate 1946. What started as a study of a single aircraft has grown into a roster of more than 300 aircraft, 229 of them flyable.

A community-made update to version 4.12.2 adds widescreen support, extra aircraft, maps and scenarios, including new maps for Kiev, Manchuria and Burma. Multiplayer options include LAN co-op and PvP alongside the solo game. It suits dedicated flight sim fans who want an enormous range of aircraft and theaters of war.
