About Ultra Street Fighter® IV

This expanded edition of Capcom's one-on-one fighting game adds five fighters and six stages, tweaks the balance of the gameplay and introduces some original modes. The costumes released earlier as downloadable content for the previous edition are also included. Fights are duels where timing, spacing and special moves decide the outcome.

You can play alone or against other people in player-versus-player matches, and the game supports controllers fully. It suits fighting game fans who enjoy learning character matchups, practicing combos and competing, as well as returning players who want a larger roster to explore.
