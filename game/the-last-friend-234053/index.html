About The Last Friend

This post-apocalyptic mix of brawler and tower defense is built around dogs, which are mysteriously vanishing while humanity is busy with mutants and survival. You play Alpha, who discovers he can understand a rescued pup named T. Juan, and together they fight through hordes of enemies and bosses in search of answers about the vanished dogs.

Rescued dogs of different breeds join your side, each with its own traits; the Newfoundland, for example, has a healing passive. A second player can join on the same screen, and full controller support is included. The tone is playful and pun-heavy, so it suits dog lovers and couch co-op duos who want action with light strategy.
