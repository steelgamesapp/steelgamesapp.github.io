About Desperados III

Desperados III is a story-driven tactical stealth game from Mimimi Games set in a brutal Wild West. You lead a band of outlaws led by John Cooper as they take on a ruthless land company, using careful plans to take down enemies.

Each character has unique skills, and the Showdown mode lets you pause time to set up several actions and trigger them all at once. Levels are open, with many ways to reach your goal, and replaying them unlocks badges and challenges. It is a hardcore single-player game for patient strategy fans.
