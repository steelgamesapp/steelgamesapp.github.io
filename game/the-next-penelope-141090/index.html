About The Next Penelope

Penelope pilots a spaceship across the galaxy in search of Ulysses in this fast action racing game, a science-fiction take on the Odyssey. Planets can be tackled in whatever order the player prefers. Each world has its own races, unusual situations and bosses, and finishing them unlocks permanent abilities that strengthen the ship.

Weapons can be combined and used at any moment, but every ability drains energy, so power and risk go hand in hand. The controls will feel familiar to anyone who has played Micro Machines, and a second scheme exists for newcomers. The look is loud and colourful. It supports single-player and multiplayer, and suits players who enjoy arcade racing with a risk-and-reward twist.
