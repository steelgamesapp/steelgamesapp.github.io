About Wavetale

Sigrid forms an unlikely friendship with a shadow whose gift lets her run across the surface of the sea. She races across the open water around Strandville, a decaying archipelago and submerged city, and swings between rooftops with her net. Her task is to protect the islanders from sea monsters and confront an old enemy.

The islanders are a varied cast, including fishermen who have had enough and secretive hermits, and Sigrid's cranky grandmother offers help along the way. The story deals with loss and reconciliation, and the surf-style traversal keeps the pace quick. Single-player, with full controller support, and suited to players who like movement-focused adventures with a warm, emotional core.
