About Assassin's Creed® Origins

Ancient Egypt is the setting for this action-adventure, with deserts, oases, the Nile, the Mediterranean coast and the tombs of Giza all part of a large open world. You play Bayek, whose story leads to the beginnings of the Assassin's Brotherhood, and you take on quests, hostile factions and wild animals along the way.

Combat moves toward RPG design, with looted weapons of different rarity and characteristics, a deeper progression system and powerful bosses. It is a single-player game that suits fans of open-world action RPGs who are interested in historical settings.
