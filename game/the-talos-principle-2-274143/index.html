About The Talos Principle 2

The Talos Principle 2 is a first-person puzzle game from Croteam. You play a robot in a future where humanity is gone, exploring a mysterious megastructure on a remote island and the stunning landscapes around it.

Puzzles grow increasingly mind-bending, using tools such as connectors, drills and gravity devices. The story raises big questions about humanity, progress and belief, discussed with a cast of companions. It greatly expands on the first game and suits fans of puzzles and philosophy.
