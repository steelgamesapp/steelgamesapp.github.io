About Floppy Knights

Turn-based tactics and card-game deckbuilding are combined here. You follow Phoebe, a young inventor saving cash to leave her parents' house, and her robot-arm friend Carlton as they take odd jobs. Her latest invention projects knights from floppy disks onto the battlefield. Before each fight you choose which knights to bring and build a deck around them, then play cards each turn to carry out your plan.

Mixing and matching card combos is the heart of the game, and different knights support different playstyles. Floppy Knights has bright art and a friendly tone, is single-player and offers full controller support, so it suits anyone who enjoys deckbuilders or tactics games.
