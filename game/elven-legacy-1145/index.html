About Elven Legacy

Elven Legacy is a turn-based fantasy strategy game and the sequel to Fantasy Wars. Its campaign continues the earlier story, following the elves after a human mage discovers a dangerous secret tied to their ancient history. Missions move across hex-based maps where you command an army of different units and heroes.

Units gain experience and can be carried from one mission to the next, so keeping your best troops alive matters. Finishing missions quickly earns extra rewards, adding a layer of pressure. It offers a single-player campaign and multiplayer battles. Elven Legacy suits players who like classic war games with a fantasy setting and careful tactical play.
