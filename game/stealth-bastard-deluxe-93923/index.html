About Stealth Bastard Deluxe

Most stealth games ask for patience, but Stealth Bastard Deluxe asks for speed. The player guides a defenseless infiltrator, equipped with nothing but a pair of stealth goggles, through a hostile facility. The character carries no weapons and wears no armor, a single sighting is fatal, and survival depends on staying in shadow and moving between hiding places at the right moments.

Each room works as a compact puzzle of timing and positioning, with traps and patrolling machinery to read and avoid. Deluxe is an expanded version of the original Stealth Bastard. It is a single-player game that suits fans of precise, trial-and-error platformers who like some stealth in the mix.
