About Bayonetta

Bayonetta is a stylish action game from PlatinumGames, directed by Hideki Kamiya. You play Bayonetta, a witch with guns on her hands and feet, fighting through hordes of angels as she tries to recover her lost memories.

Combat is fast and flashy, built on combos, magic attacks and Witch Time, a slow-motion effect you trigger by dodging at the last moment. Ranks at the end of each fight push you to play with more style. The PC version runs at 60 frames per second at high resolutions, the best way to play this classic.
