About Make Way

Course building is the hook in Make Way, a top-down party racer in the spirit of Micro Machines and Mashed. Between rounds, everyone picks pieces such as seesaws, loops, corkscrews and train crossings and snaps them onto the track. Then the cars race, dodging hazards, firing weapons and trying not to fall off the edge.

Courses keep growing until someone wins, and then a fresh one begins. You can cooperate on the layout or use it to sabotage rivals, and the chaotic tone suits groups of friends. Online player-versus-player matches and a solo mode are available, with full controller support. It fits players who prefer noisy, competitive racing to realistic simulation.
