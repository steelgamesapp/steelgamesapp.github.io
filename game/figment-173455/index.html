About Figment

Inside a human mind, a surreal place built from thoughts, memories and inner voices, nightmarish creatures have started to spread fear after years of calm. You guide Dusty, the mind's grumpy former voice of courage, and his upbeat friend Piper through its different regions, solving puzzles, fighting off nightmares and trying to bring back the lost courage.

Music is central to this action-adventure, and humor lightens a story about facing fears. It is a single-player game with full controller support, suited to players who enjoy imaginative, colorful worlds and a mix of light puzzles and combat.
