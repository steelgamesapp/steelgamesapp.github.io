About Soulstice

Soulstice is a third-person action game set in a dark fantasy world. You control two sisters at once: Briar, a powerful warrior known as a Chimera, and Lute, the spirit of her sister bound to her, who supports her with defensive and supernatural abilities. Together they push through ruined lands overrun by monsters while their own story comes of age.

It is a single-player character-action game in the style of Devil May Cry or Bayonetta, with combos, multiple weapons and large boss battles. It suits players who enjoy mastering a deep combat system and learning to manage two characters at the same time.
