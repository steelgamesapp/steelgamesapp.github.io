About Planet of Lana

Lana and her animal companion Mui live on a planet that was once in balance between people, nature and wildlife. That balance has broken: a faceless machine army has arrived, and the pair must lean on each other to survive and protect the people they love in this cinematic puzzle platformer.

The companion shapes how puzzles work, and the game mixes them with stealth and reflex-driven action sequences. Its story spans galaxies and centuries yet stays focused on hope, loss and love rather than war. Single-player, with full controller support, and suited to players who enjoy emotional, atmospheric adventures.
