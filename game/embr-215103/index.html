About Embr

Rushing into burning buildings is the job in this chaotic firefighting game, where corporate crews put out flames, save people and salvage valuables. The 25 levels span three districts, and each is filled with hazards such as electrical faults, gas leaks and security systems you may need to evade. Earnings buy better tools, upgrades and outfits.

Embr can be played alone or with up to four players online, with difficulty adjusting to the size of the crew. LAN co-op and daily challenges are included. The tone is frantic and light, so it suits friends who like cooperative games where plans fall apart.
