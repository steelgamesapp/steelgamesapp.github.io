About Nobody Wants to Die

New York, 2329: technology offers eternal life, with consciousness kept in memory banks or moved from body to body for those who can pay. You play Detective James Karra of the Mortality Department, who investigates a case his chief hands him unofficially, with police liaison Sara Kai as his only help.

The case centers on a serial killer picking off members of the elite in the city, and advanced technology helps the pair dig up uncomfortable secrets about the powerful. It is a single-player interactive noir story with a serious, moody tone, aimed at fans of detective fiction and science fiction who like thinking about the cost of immortality. Full controller support is included.
