About The Coma 2: Vicious Sisters

Waking at night inside Sehwa High, student Mina Park finds her school warped into something sinister. Someone who resembles her teacher pursues her, so she must leave the building and cross the surrounding Sehwa district, solving puzzles, finding clues and meeting strange creatures and uncertain allies on the way.

This horror adventure from Korea is atmospheric and story-driven, and survival in The Coma 2 depends on evading a relentless threat while making use of whatever resources can be found. It is single-player and suits horror fans who enjoy narrative adventures set in a Korean school and neighborhood.
