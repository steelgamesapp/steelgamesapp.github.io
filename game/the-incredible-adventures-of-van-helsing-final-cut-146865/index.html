About The Incredible Adventures of Van Helsing: Final Cut

Van Helsing and his ghostly companion, Lady Katarina, explore Borgovia, a gothic-noir setting where experimental science disturbs the balance between monsters and humans. The path leads from wilderness to the dark districts of a city warped by mad science.

Van Helsing: Final Cut gathers three stand-alone episodes into one continuous story, with a campaign of more than 50 hours, six playable classes, and an endgame mode added for this edition that offers many open missions. Developed by NeocoreGames, it can be played solo or in co-op. It suits action RPG fans who enjoy loot, character builds and a gothic setting.
