About Call of Juarez: Gunslinger

Call of Juarez: Gunslinger frames a first-person shooter as a series of tall tales. Silas Greaves, a bounty hunter in his later years, recounts the outlaws he says he hunted down, and the levels take the player from dusty mining country to saloon interiors. Jesse James, Pat Garrett and Billy the Kid figure among the legends in his account, and the missions revolve around gunfights and quick-draw duels.

The narration is part of the design, because the storyteller's telling shapes what happens on screen. Techland built it as a single-player western with full controller support, aimed at players who want a stylized, violent shooter rooted in Wild West folklore.
