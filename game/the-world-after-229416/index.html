About The World After

This full-motion video adventure takes place in a French village during a pandemic lockdown. You follow a man troubled by strange recurring nightmares, and as he digs into their meaning he begins to suspect that the village he lives in is hiding something. The story is told through live-action footage with real actors, and your decisions guide where the investigation goes.

This single-player game works like an interactive film, so the focus is on watching scenes, picking options and piecing together the mystery rather than on reflexes or puzzles. The pace is slow and atmospheric. The World After suits players who enjoy FMV games, psychological mysteries and stories that unfold like a television drama.
