About Volume

Volume is a stealth game from Bithell Games, whose earlier work includes Thomas Was Alone. It reimagines the Robin Hood story in a near future: Rob Locksley uses a simulation device to stream heists that show people how to steal from the rich and expose a corrupt ruler named Gisborne. Levels are clean, top-down virtual spaces where you dodge patrolling guards and collect gems.

Play is about patterns, sightlines and distractions, and you can retry instantly, so each stage becomes a neat stealth puzzle. It is single-player, and a built-in level editor lets players make and share their own stages. Volume suits players who prefer thoughtful stealth over action, and who like short, replayable levels.
