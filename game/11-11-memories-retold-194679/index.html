About 11-11 Memories Retold

11-11 Memories Retold follows two men on opposite sides of the First World War, starting in November 1916. Harry is a young Canadian photographer who signs up and heads for the western front, while Kurt is a German engineer who learns that his son has been reported missing and goes looking for him. Made by Aardman Animations with Digixart, the story moves between the two as their paths draw closer.

The game is a single-player narrative adventure with a painterly visual style, and its pace is slow and reflective rather than combat focused. It suits players who enjoy story-led games about ordinary people caught up in history, and who are happy to explore, take photographs and make quiet choices.
