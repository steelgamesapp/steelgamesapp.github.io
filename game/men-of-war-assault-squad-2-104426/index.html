About Men of War: Assault Squad 2

Men of War: Assault Squad 2 is a real-time tactics game set in World War II. You command infantry, tanks and artillery across five factions, with detailed units that can take cover, use equipment and fight realistically.

Direct Control lets you take over any unit and aim and drive yourself. There are 65 maps, hundreds of vehicles and soldiers, skirmish missions and massive multiplayer battles, including co-op. It suits players who want a deep and demanding WWII tactics game.
