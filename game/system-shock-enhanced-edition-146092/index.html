About System Shock: Enhanced Edition

System Shock: Enhanced Edition rebuilds the original first-person immersive sim from its source code, a project handled by Nightdive Studios. The game mixes action, adventure and RPG elements, and casts you aboard the space station Citadel against the hostile AI SHODAN.

Upgrades include resolutions up to 4K, mouselook, rebindable controls, an adjustable field of view and a choice between Direct3D and OpenGL, plus an option to use either the newer Mac artwork or the old DOS artwork. Fan-made missions and mods are officially supported. It is single-player, and it suits players curious about the roots of immersive sims who are willing to accept an older design.
