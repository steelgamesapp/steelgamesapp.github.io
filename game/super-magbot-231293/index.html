About Super Magbot

Jumping is off the table in this precision 2D platformer. Instead, the robot hero Magbot controls red positive and blue negative magnetic polarity, attracting himself toward platforms to cross gaps and repelling off surfaces to launch through the air. The goal is to rescue his homeworld, MagTek, from a villain tearing through the galaxy, and levels demand speed and accuracy.

Star fragments in hard-to-reach spots unlock extra challenges, and global leaderboards invite time-based competition. The 16-bit style visuals of Super Magbot recall classic console platformers. A single-player game with controller support, it suits players who enjoy mastering movement mechanics and replaying levels to improve.
