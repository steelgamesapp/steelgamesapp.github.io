About Deathtrap

Strongholds built long ago to keep otherworldly monsters at bay are under attack again as creatures pour back into the world. You pick a hero and defend the forts by using, rebuilding and upgrading old traps and magical contraptions.

The game mixes tower defense with action RPG elements, so you fight on the battlefield alongside your traps instead of only placing them. Whirling blades, deadly machines and plenty of gore set the tone. The single-player campaign is the centerpiece, with multiplayer also supported and full controller support. It suits tower defense fans who want to be in the thick of the fight.
