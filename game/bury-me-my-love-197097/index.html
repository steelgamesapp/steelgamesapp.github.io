About Bury Me, My Love

This game plays out as a text-message conversation. You are Majd, who has stayed behind in Syria while his wife Nour, a migrant, travels toward Europe. As the two chat on a messaging app much like WhatsApp, you advise her as best you can, and the path she takes depends partly on what you suggest. The story follows her perilous journey as she tries to reach safety.

The title is a Syrian farewell that roughly means you must not die before the speaker does, and it gains weight when Majd says it to Nour. Co-produced by ARTE, Figs and The Pixel Hunt, it is single-player and serious in tone.
