About We Are The Dwarves

Three dwarven astronauts are the heroes of We Are The Dwarves, a tactical adventure played in real time. In their universe, stars sit inside stone, and the trio head into the depths to find new light for their people. Each dwarf has a distinct role and skill set, and you guide them through hostile caverns full of creatures that punish careless moves.

Combat runs in real time but can be paused or slowed to give orders, so it rewards planning, stealth and careful use of abilities. It is a single-player game with a stiff difficulty level. It suits players who enjoy squad tactics in the style of Commandos or Baldur's Gate and do not mind a challenge.
