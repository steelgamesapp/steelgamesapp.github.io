About EVERSPACE™

Space combat meets roguelike structure in this single-player shooter. You fly a ship through a changing universe, shooting enemies, crafting and looting as you try to survive against heavy odds. Every run presents new situations, and death is not the end, since it leads into a larger journey.

A story about the pilot's own existence emerges through meetings with characters who each hold a different piece of it. Rockfish Games developed EVERSPACE, and it puts weight on its visuals, with VR support listed alongside full controller support. It suits players who like roguelike repetition, improvisation and space dogfights.
