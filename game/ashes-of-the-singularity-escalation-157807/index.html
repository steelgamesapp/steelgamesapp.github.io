About Ashes of the Singularity: Escalation

This RTS is built around scale: huge numbers of units can be active at once as you run an army across maps big enough to cover entire worlds. Two factions, humanity's Post-Human Coalition and the Substrate, fight for the galaxy, and the story pits humanity against the new threat while rebel humans stake out their own territory.

Single-player campaigns cover several worlds, and skirmishes can be played online against opponents or alongside allies. The engine was designed for modern multi-core processors, and the game became a common benchmark for DirectX 12 performance. It is best for strategy players who like large-scale macro management over commanding a handful of heroes.
