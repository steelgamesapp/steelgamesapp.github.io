About Sherlock Holmes The Awakened

Sherlock Holmes The Awakened is a detective adventure from Frogwares, rebuilt from the ground up with modern graphics and gameplay. Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson investigate mysterious disappearances that lead them into the terrifying Cthulhu Mythos.

You gather clues, reconstruct events and connect evidence to make your own deductions, while the story grows darker as Holmes faces things beyond logic. It is a single-player game for fans of detective stories and Lovecraftian horror.
