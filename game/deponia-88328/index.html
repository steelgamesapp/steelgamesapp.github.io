About Deponia

Rufus, a scheming inventor from the lowest social class, is stuck on Deponia, a world buried in rubbish, and dreams of escaping to Elysium, the floating city of the wealthy. When a woman named Goal drops from above into his trash pile, he sees a way up and sets out to return her, kicking off a string of ill-advised plans. The game is a comedy adventure with a love story at its center.

Play follows the point-and-click tradition: explore hand-drawn scenes, collect items and combine them to get past absurd obstacles. The humor is sarcastic and the lead a self-serving anti-hero, so it suits fans of classic LucasArts-style comedy adventures. It is single-player.
