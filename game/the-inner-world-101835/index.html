About The Inner World

The Inner World is a hand-drawn point-and-click adventure set in Asposia, an unusual world enclosed by an endless mass of earth. The winds that once blew through it have vanished, and Robert, a naive young court musician with a flute for a nose, leaves his sheltered life to find out why. Along the way he teams up with Laura, a quick-witted thief whose motives are not entirely clear.

It plays like a classic adventure: talk to odd characters, collect items and combine them to solve puzzles, with a built-in hint system if you get stuck. It is single-player and relaxed in pace, with plenty of dry humor. The Inner World suits fans of traditional point-and-click games.
