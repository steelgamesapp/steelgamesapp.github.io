About Fahrenheit: Indigo Prophecy Remastered

Fahrenheit: Indigo Prophecy Remastered opens in a New York diner, where Lucas Kane comes out of a trance to find he has just killed a stranger. The story follows him as he tries to understand what happened, while detectives Carla Valenti and Tyler Miles work the same case from the other side. You switch between these characters, make choices in conversation and get through tense moments with timed button prompts.

Aspyr's remaster adds sharper HD textures and better controller handling, and it uses the full international cut of the original 2005 game. It is a single-player story game with light adventure elements, best for players who enjoy a cinematic thriller and do not mind trading deep mechanics for drama.
