About WRATH: Aeon of Ruin

WRATH: Aeon of Ruin is a hardcore first-person shooter inspired by the icons of the 90s and built on the legendary Quake engine. Taking place in a realm left to rot, you hunt down the guardians of a dying world.

You blast, slice and shred through enemies with powerful weapons and artifacts, while exploring large, interconnected levels full of secrets. Saving is limited, so careful play matters. It suits fans of classic shooters.
