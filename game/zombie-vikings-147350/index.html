About Zombie Vikings

Four undead Norse warriors, among them Gunborg and Hedgy, are sent to recover Odin's lost eye in this brawler for one to four players. The adventure runs through more than 25 levels in eight worlds, taking in a gingerbread swamp and the inside of the Midgaard Serpent, with five versus arenas on the side.

Each Viking has unique power moves, and over 40 unlockable weapons leave plenty of room to experiment. Zach Weinersmith of SMBC Comics wrote the story, and the tone is absurd and comic. Zoink Games developed it. You can play alone, or with friends online or offline, which suits groups looking for a silly co-op brawler.
