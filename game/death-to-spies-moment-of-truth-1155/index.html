About Death to Spies: Moment of Truth

Death to Spies: Moment of Truth is the sequel to Death to Spies and continues the story of Soviet counterintelligence during World War II. You again take the role of a SMERSH agent, carrying out covert operations against German forces and enemy agents in a series of standalone missions.

Played from a third-person view, it focuses on disguises, careful movement and choosing when to act. Missions reward patience and planning, and loud approaches tend to go badly. It is single-player only and suits players who enjoy older-style stealth action with a historical setting. Anyone who liked the first game will find more of the same approach here, with new locations to infiltrate.
