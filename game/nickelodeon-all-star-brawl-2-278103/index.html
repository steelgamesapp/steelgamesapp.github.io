About Nickelodeon All-Star Brawl 2

Nickelodeon All-Star Brawl 2 is a platform fighter with characters from Nickelodeon shows such as SpongeBob SquarePants, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles and Avatar: The Last Airbender. Matches take place on multi-level stages where you knock opponents off the screen, and each fighter has a distinct move set plus a Super attack that can turn a match around. There is also a single-player campaign with roguelike elements.

You can play against the computer, compete with friends locally or take your skills online. Matches are short and fast, and newcomers can pick it up while experienced players can dig into movement and combos. It suits fans of these shows and of platform fighters in general.
