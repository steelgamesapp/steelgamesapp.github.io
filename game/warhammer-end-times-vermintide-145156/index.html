About Warhammer: End Times - Vermintide

Ubersreik has been overrun by Skaven, the rat-men of the Warhammer Fantasy setting, and Warhammer: End Times - Vermintide has a team of up to four heroes fight through the swarm across a series of missions. It is a cooperative action game combining first-person melee and ranged combat, with locations ranging from the heights of Magnus Tower to the Under Empire below. Players choose from five heroes, each with different abilities, gear and personality.

Teamwork is central, since the swarms punish players who stray from the group. Friends can join online, and AI companions can fill empty slots. It suits fans of cooperative horde games who prefer melee-heavy combat to long-range shooting.
