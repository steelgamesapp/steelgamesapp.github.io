About LEGO® Batman™: The Videogame

After the villains of Arkham Asylum break out together, Batman and Robin take on the job of rounding them up across a brick-built Gotham City. LEGO Batman: The Videogame turns that chase into a lighthearted action adventure, with fights, vehicle sections and small puzzles solved by constructing objects out of bricks and switching between gadgets and suits. Targets include the Joker, the Penguin and the Scarecrow.

A second campaign flips the perspective and lets the player take control of the villains, and two people can play together. The humor and approachable action make it a natural fit for families, younger players and fans of the Dark Knight who enjoy a lighter take on Gotham.
