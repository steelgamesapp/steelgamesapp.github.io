About Indivisible

Ajna is a headstrong young woman raised by her father at the edge of a rural town, until an attack on her home awakens a mysterious power inside her. She embarks on a journey across a large fantasy world to protect everything she knows.

Along the way she meets many Incarnations, people who can join her fight, and the roster grows to dozens of playable characters. Lab Zero Games, the studio behind Skullgirls, hand-drew the art and animation for this action RPG platformer, which mixes platforming with real-time combat and draws on many cultures and mythologies. Shared or split-screen co-op is supported alongside single-player.
