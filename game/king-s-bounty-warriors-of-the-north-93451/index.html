About King's Bounty: Warriors of the North

Olaf, a young Viking whose father is Tormund the Fierce, leads the defense of the north of Endoria against an undead army spreading toward the kingdom's heart. This tactical RPG is a standalone follow-up to The Legend, set years after that game's events, and it gives him Rune Magic and Valkyries to command.

The campaign takes Olaf across frozen islands, old fortresses and ravaged lands to liberate Nordig. As in the series, you explore the map and fight turn-based battles with an army under your command. It suits strategy-RPG fans who enjoy building an army and growing a hero. Single-player.
