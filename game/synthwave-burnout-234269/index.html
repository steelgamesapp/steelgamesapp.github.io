About Synthwave Burnout

Neon highways inspired by 1980s science fiction set the stage for this arcade racer. At the wheel of a sci-fi supercar, you gather momentum and charge energy while a synthwave soundtrack drives the pace, so music, motion and visuals blend into one continuous run.

Rivals have real physical presence, so they can block overtakes, shove you off your line and cause collisions, which turns every race into a fight for control. Speed is the key resource, so the driving rewards aggression and flow. Single-player, with full controller support, and aimed at arcade racing fans who value atmosphere.
