About Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden

Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden is a tactical game set on an Earth where humanity has collapsed. You lead a small team of mutants, including a duck named Dux and a boar named Bormin, out from their settlement, the Ark, to scavenge the dangerous Zone. Exploration happens in real time, and when a fight starts, the game switches to turn-based combat in the style of XCOM.

Stealth matters a lot, since you can pick off enemies quietly or set up ambushes before a battle. It is a single-player game with a fixed story, a measured pace and a fairly demanding difficulty. It suits players who enjoy tactics games and want more story and exploration between battles.
