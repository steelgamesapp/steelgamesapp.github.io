About Expeditions: Viking

Expeditions: Viking is a party-based role-playing game set around 790 AD. You are the new chieftain of a small Norse clan, and you lead a band of warriors across the sea to Britain in search of wealth and glory.

Combat is turn-based and tactical, and your choices shape alliances, rivalries and the fate of your people back home. You manage your camp, heal wounds and keep your followers happy. It is a single-player game for players who want a historical RPG with real consequences for their decisions.
