About Star Overdrive

Star Overdrive is an open-world action adventure set on an alien planet. You travel across the landscape on a hoverboard, building speed and pulling off tricks, and you use special powers to fight enemies, solve environmental puzzles and reach new areas. Exploration plays a large part, with the open world encouraging you to wander and discover.

It is a single-player game from Italian studio Caracal Games, published by Dear Villagers. Moving at speed is the main pleasure, mixed with lighter combat and puzzle-solving. It suits players who like freedom to explore and enjoy movement-focused games with some challenge.
