About Blacksad: Under the Skin

Set in 1950s New York, Blacksad: Under the Skin casts you as John Blacksad, a private detective drawn from a comic book series. A boxing club owner has died, and his daughter Sonia, now running the financially troubled gym, hires Blacksad to find Bobby Yale, a rising fighter who vanished before the biggest match of his career. The case leads through the city's grim underworld.

The characters are animal-like figures, which keeps the game close to its comic source while the mood stays that of a noir detective novel. It is a single-player adventure with full controller support, suited to players who prefer a mystery driven by investigation and conversation over fast action.
