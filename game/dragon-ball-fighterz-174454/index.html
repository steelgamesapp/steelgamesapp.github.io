About DRAGON BALL FighterZ

Fights in DRAGON BALL FighterZ are built around teams of three. Developed with Arc System Works, it is an anime-styled fighting game in which you pick three fighters from the Dragon Ball roster, tag them in and out, and use support moves to chain high-speed combos. Unreal Engine visuals give the battles a polished, animated look.

Online options include ranked matches, an interactive lobby and a six-player Party Match. A story mode features Android 21, a character created for the game. With both single-player and multiplayer modes and full controller support, it suits Dragon Ball fans and anyone who wants fast, flashy matches that reward practice.
