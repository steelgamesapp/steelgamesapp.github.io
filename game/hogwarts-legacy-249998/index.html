About Hogwarts Legacy

The wizarding world of the Harry Potter books is revisited in the 1800s, well before the stories most people know, in this open-world action RPG. You play a student tied to an old secret that could divide the wizarding world. You attend classes, explore the castle and its surroundings, make allies and confront Dark wizards.

Along the way you cast and master spells, craft potions, find magical beasts, upgrade talents and customize your character. This version also includes the Haunted Hogsmeade Shop Quest, with an extra dungeon and a cosmetic set. It is single-player with full controller support, and it suits fans of the Harry Potter setting and anyone who likes exploring a large magical world.
