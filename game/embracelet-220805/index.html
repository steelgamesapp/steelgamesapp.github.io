About Embracelet

Jesper, a seventeen-year-old from the city, inherits a magical bracelet from his grandfather that gives him telekinetic powers. He travels to the dwindling fishing village of Slepp, on an island in Northern Norway, to learn where the bracelet came from and why his grandfather left. Light physics puzzles ask you to move objects, repair machinery and help the quirky locals.

The island is rendered in a calm low-poly style, and the puzzles are gentle, leaving room for the coming-of-age story centered on family, friendship and love. Your choices influence how that story unfolds. Single-player, with full controller support, and a good pick for players who prefer a warm, reflective adventure to a demanding one.
