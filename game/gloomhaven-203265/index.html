About Gloomhaven

Isaac Childres' board game gets a digital adaptation in this tactical RPG, where you lead a band of mercenaries in a harsh medieval dark-fantasy setting. You take jobs from anyone who pays, whether city officials or cultists, then fight through dungeons, forests and caves in turn-based battles driven by ability cards, with every choice mattering.

Flaming Fowl Studios and Saber Interactive handle the adaptation, with solo play and online co-op, plus full controller support. It suits tactics fans who like careful planning and mercenary-for-hire storytelling, and board game fans who want it without the setup.
