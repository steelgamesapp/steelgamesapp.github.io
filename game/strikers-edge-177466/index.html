About Strikers Edge

Dodgeball goes medieval here, with warriors hurling weapons at each other across a rift. You pick one of eight strikers, each with its own abilities, stats and weapon, and fight in one of four arenas whose layouts, obstacles and hazards change the tactics. Accuracy, anticipating your opponent's throws and good timing decide matches.

The game is built for local multiplayer, with split-screen play for both head-to-head and cooperative matches, and a ladder to climb. It suits groups sharing a couch and players who enjoy learning the differences between characters and outwitting a human opponent.
