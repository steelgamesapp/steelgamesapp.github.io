About Rogue Lords

The Devil is your character in this dark fantasy roguelike, back from hell and seeking vengeance against the Demon Hunters. Each run you lead a team of disciples, drawn from famous villains, through story events and turn-based battles, unlocking skills and building synergies between them. Collecting ancient artifacts is the route to your revenge.

Being the Devil means you can bend the rules of Rogue Lords itself at critical moments, which sets it apart from other turn-based roguelikes. Single-player with full controller support, it suits strategy fans who enjoy repeatable runs, team synergies and a villainous protagonist.
