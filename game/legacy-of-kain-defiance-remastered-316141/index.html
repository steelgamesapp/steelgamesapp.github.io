About Legacy of Kain: Defiance Remastered

Legacy of Kain: Defiance Remastered is a remastered version of the 2003 action adventure. You play both the vampire Kain and his betrayed lieutenant Raziel as their paths collide across the dark world of Nosgoth.

You wield legendary blades, use supernatural abilities and fight through a world of conflict and prophecy. The remaster updates the visuals while keeping the original's story and voice acting. It suits longtime fans and newcomers curious about this classic series.
