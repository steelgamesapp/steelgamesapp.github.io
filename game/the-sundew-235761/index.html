About The Sundew

The Sundew is a pixel-art point-and-click adventure set in a bleak future. You play Anna Isobe, a police officer with cybernetic implants who has been left behind as the world moves on, and who stumbles into a conspiracy that reaches far beyond her own city. The story travels across several locations and asks you to make decisions that change how events unfold and how the story ends.

It plays like a traditional adventure, with conversations, item puzzles and investigation, at a measured pace suited to reading and exploring. It is a single-player game. The Sundew suits players who enjoy cyberpunk fiction and classic adventures in the style of the 1990s, especially those who like stories with choices that carry weight.
