About Under The Waves

Stan is a professional diver working in the North Sea in an alternate, technologically advanced 1970s. He is coping with a loss that changed his life, and the isolation of long underwater assignments mirrors his state of mind. Strange events begin to unfold beneath the surface as you guide him through his work, and he eventually has to make a major decision.

Grief is the central theme, handled through cinematic visuals and a poetic tone, with the ocean depicted as both beautiful and lonely. This is a single-player narrative adventure with full controller support, suited to players who like emotional, story-led games set deep underwater.
