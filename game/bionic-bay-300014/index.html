About Bionic Bay

Armed with a strange teleportation device, a scientist is trying to leave a biomechanical world that is old, hazardous and full of secrets. Bionic Bay is a physics-driven platformer built around a swap ability, which lets you trade places with objects and enemies or shift gravity, using the environment to move, defend and attack.

Realistic physics means fire, ice, breakage and explosions can kill you, so experiments have consequences. The pixel art is dense and gritty, and the sound design builds suspense in each biome. Movement is built with speedrunning in mind. Single-player, with full controller support, and best for players who like precise platforming and creative problem solving.
