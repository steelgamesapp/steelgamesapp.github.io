About Rebel Galaxy

At the frontier of explored space, you captain a powerful star destroyer and decide what kind of spacefarer to be. You battle pirates, mine asteroids, scavenge wreckage, trade goods, investigate anomalies and deal with some outlandish aliens.

Playing as a do-gooder, a trader or a privateer, or a mix of all three, you earn credits to buy bigger ships and fit them with weapons and defenses. Ship combat has a naval flavor, and the tone is playful and swashbuckling. It is a single-player space adventure with full controller support, suited to players who like open-ended combat and trading.
