About Down in Bermuda

Stranded for decades in the Bermuda after an unnatural storm, aviator Milton must find a way home in this quirky puzzle adventure. Caught in a bubble of time, he relies on you to escape each of six islands, where you crack codes, solve puzzles and collect magic orbs. Every island leads to the next as the search continues.

Along the way you deal with sea creatures and uncover secrets about the islands. Down in Bermuda comes from Yak & Co, the studio behind Agent A, and keeps a light, offbeat approach. Single-player with full controller support, it suits puzzle fans who want something relaxed and inventive.
