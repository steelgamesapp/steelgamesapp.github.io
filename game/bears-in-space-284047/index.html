About Bears In Space

Maxwell Atoms, a spacetronaut marooned far from Earth, has had his DNA merged with that of a she-bear, and in this bullet-hell first-person shooter he can transform into a bear to fight. Your aim is to get home by blasting through robots hungry for oil across wastelands, space carnivals and ancient temples.

The tone is zany and retro-futuristic, with a large arsenal of outlandish weapons and gadgets, plus double jumps and dashes that keep the action moving. Fights favor constant motion over cover. It is single-player with full controller support, aimed at players who want loud, quick, tongue-in-cheek shooting.
