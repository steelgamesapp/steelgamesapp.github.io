About HyperParasite

HyperParasite is a roguelite twin-stick game set in an alternate 1980s, where an alien parasite tries to take over humanity. On its own, the parasite is weak, so you take over the bodies of people you meet, each with their own weapons and abilities. There are 60 characters to unlock, and the ultimate goal is to work up the ladder, all the way to possessing the President.

Each run is randomly generated and can end quickly if you are careless, so it rewards quick reactions and choosing hosts well. You can play alone or with a friend in local co-op. HyperParasite suits players who like fast, replayable shooters with a sense of humor.
