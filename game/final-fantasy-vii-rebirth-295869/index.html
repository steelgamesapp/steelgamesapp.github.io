About FINAL FANTASY VII REBIRTH

Cloud and his companions leave Midgar behind and head across the wider world in this second part of the three-game remake of Final Fantasy VII. You explore a large open world, gallop over grasslands on a chocobo and follow the story as the Shinra company keeps draining the planet's lifestream.

Combat blends real-time action with menu-driven commands. The PC release adds options such as NVIDIA DLSS, variable refresh rate and DualSense controller support. It is a single-player game with full controller support, best suited to RPG fans following the remake trilogy.
