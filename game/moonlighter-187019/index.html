About Moonlighter

Will keeps a shop in Rynoka, a village built near a set of ancient Gates that lead to other realms. He secretly wants to be a hero, so he takes his sword into the dungeons beyond the Gates and hauls treasure back to sell. The game splits its time between those two jobs: action combat with rogue-lite elements below ground, and shopkeeping above it.

What you find decides what you can sell and what gear you can afford. The current version adds Companions, extra mini-bosses and gear, more than 100 additional room layouts and a New Game+ option. Single-player with full controller support, it suits fans of dungeon crawling that feeds a small shop sim.
