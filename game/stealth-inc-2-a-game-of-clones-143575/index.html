About Stealth Inc 2: A Game of Clones

As a clone, you flee a sinister, high-tech testing facility laid out as a Metroidvania-style overworld of five areas and 60 levels. You rescue fellow clones as you go, dodging deadly lasers, killer robots and bosses.

Death is not permanent for a clone. There are no loading screens or lives to lose, so learning from each failure is part of the design. The game asks for quick reflexes as well as planning, and it is single-player. It suits stealth-platformer fans who enjoy trial and error and exploring a connected map.
