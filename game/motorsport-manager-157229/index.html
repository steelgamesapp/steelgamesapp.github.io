About Motorsport Manager

Motorsport Manager puts you in charge of a racing team, from hiring drivers and engineers to designing and improving your cars. On race weekends, you plan practice and qualifying, then call strategy live during the race, deciding when to pit, which tires to fit and how hard drivers should push as weather and conditions change. Between races, you manage budgets, sponsors and car development.

It is a single-player management game that can be as deep as you want, with many seasons of play. Races can be sped up or followed closely. Motorsport Manager suits fans of motorsport who enjoy strategy and spreadsheets more than driving, and anyone who liked classic sports management games.
