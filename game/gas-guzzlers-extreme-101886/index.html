About Gas Guzzlers Extreme

In the combat racing game Gas Guzzlers Extreme, the cars carry machine guns, rockets and other weapons. You race around tracks while shooting rivals and grabbing pickups, earn money from wins and kills, and spend it on new cars and upgrades. Besides standard races, there are modes where the aim is to knock out opponents or fight in arenas instead of crossing the finish line first.

The pace is fast and loud, and it does not take itself seriously. There is a single-player career plus multiplayer and co-op modes for playing with others. It suits players who miss arcade car combat games and want quick, chaotic sessions more than realistic driving.
