About Hell is Us

Hell Is Us is an action adventure from Rogue Factor. In an isolated country torn apart by civil war, you explore to uncover the secrets of your past and deal with the effects of a mysterious calamity.

The game encourages real exploration, with no map or quest markers to guide you, so you rely on clues and your own observations. Combat is melee-focused, and you face strange creatures born from the calamity. It suits players who like atmospheric games and figuring things out for themselves.
