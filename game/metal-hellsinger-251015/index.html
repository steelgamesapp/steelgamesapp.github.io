About Metal: Hellsinger

In the rhythm-based first-person shooter Metal: Hellsinger, you play the Unknown, a half-human, half-demon who fights through the Hells to take revenge on the Red Judge. Shooting, reloading and dodging in time with the music boosts your damage and builds a multiplier, and the soundtrack grows louder as you keep the beat. Each level has its own track, with vocals from well-known metal singers.

It is single-player, with a campaign that can be finished in a few hours and challenges that extend replay value. The pace is fast, and staying on beat takes practice. Metal: Hellsinger suits players who enjoy shooters like Doom and want a twist that rewards a sense of rhythm.
