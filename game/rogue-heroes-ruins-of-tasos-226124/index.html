About Rogue Heroes: Ruins of Tasos

Ancient Titans threaten Tasos, and you set out as one of several heroes sent to stop them by clearing dungeons that are reshuffled on every run. Viewed from above in pixel art, the game borrows its feel from classic Zelda titles, with sword swings, locked doors and puzzle rooms. Each death sends you back to the start with permanent upgrades, so every attempt goes further.

Up to four players can join on the same screen or online, or you can go solo. The pace is approachable and the tone bright, a good fit for friends who want a lighthearted dungeon crawl rather than a punishing one.
