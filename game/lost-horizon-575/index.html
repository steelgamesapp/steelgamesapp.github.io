About Lost Horizon

In 1936, Fenton Paddock, a British ex-soldier turned small-time smuggler, agrees to search for Richard, a friend who vanished in Tibet. The trail carries him across several continents while German soldiers chase supernatural relics on behalf of the Nazi regime, and the two quests keep crossing paths. The setting leans on 1930s exotic locations and pulp adventure.

This is a classic point-and-click adventure built on collecting items, combining them and talking to characters to move the plot forward. Animation Arts, the studio behind the Secret Files games, developed it as a single-player experience. It appeals to players who enjoy puzzle-driven exploration, travel across varied locations and a story with an old-fashioned adventure-film tone and a hapless hero.
