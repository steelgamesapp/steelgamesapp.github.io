About Saints Row 2

Saints Row 2 is an open-world crime game from Volition. You play the leader of the Saints, who wakes from a coma and sets out to reclaim the city of Stilwater from rival gangs and corporations.

You can customize your character in detail and approach missions and activities however you want, from insurance fraud to chaotic rampages. The tone is over the top and full of humor. It suits players who want a wild, freeform open-world game.
