About Secret Files: Tunguska

Secret Files: Tunguska is a point-and-click mystery built around the real Tunguska event of 1908, when a huge explosion flattened forest across a remote part of Siberia. In the game, Nina Kalenkov finds her father, a scientist who studied the event, has vanished from his office, and her search with his colleague Max Gruber pulls them into a mystery that spans several countries.

You explore, collect and combine objects, and question characters to move the investigation along. It is a single-player adventure with a measured pace and no action, suited to players who enjoy classic puzzle-based adventures with a historical hook.
