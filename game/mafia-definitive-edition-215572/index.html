About Mafia: Definitive Edition

Mafia: Definitive Edition is a remake of the classic crime game from Hangar 13. Cab driver Tommy Angelo has a brush with the mob in the 1930s city of Lost Heaven and is drawn into the world of organized crime.

The game is story-driven, with shootouts, car chases and missions for the Salieri family. The remake has new visuals, voice acting and expanded story elements. It suits players who enjoy cinematic crime stories.
