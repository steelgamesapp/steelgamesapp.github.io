About Life is Strange: Before the Storm

Life is Strange: Before The Storm is a standalone story adventure set three years before the first Life is Strange. You play Chloe Price, a rebellious teenager who forms an unlikely friendship with Rachel Amber.

Choices shape relationships and the story, and the Backtalk system lets Chloe use her sharp tongue to win arguments. The story deals with grief, friendship and family. It is a single-player game for fans of emotional, choice-driven narratives.
