About 911 Operator

Emergency calls come in, and as the dispatcher you decide what happens next. Depending on the situation, you might talk someone through first aid, offer advice, send the right number of police, fire or ambulance units, or conclude that a call is a prank and ignore it. Callers range from desperate relatives to people just wasting your time.

Free Play lets you pick a city anywhere in the world, using its real streets, addresses and emergency infrastructure, so you can run the dispatch center for your own hometown. It is a single-player management game about triage and time pressure, suited to players who like simulation and strategy built around judgment calls.
