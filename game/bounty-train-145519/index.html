About Bounty Train

The American West of the 19th century is the backdrop for this strategy and simulation game about running a train. You acquire a legendary steam locomotive, fit it out with equipment until it becomes a fortress on rails, hire a crew, and earn a living by hauling goods and passengers across dangerous country.

Problems can be handled by talking, fighting or paying, and quests bring encounters with notorious bandits and other enemies. The game draws on accurate historic events and period inventions, which gives the management a grounded context. It is single-player, and it suits players who enjoy planning, resource management and a Wild West theme.
