About Say No! More

Saying NO to every absurd request is the whole idea behind this comedy, which bills itself as the first NPG, a No-Playing Game. You create a custom intern who starts a career in a quirky office, and your main action is shouting NO, which you can vary with different emotional tones and in several languages to knock colleagues off balance, then charge up for a stronger effect.

Controls are easy for newcomers to learn, and Say No! More follows a set path through the working day. The humor is silly and aimed at office culture. Single-player, it suits players who enjoy absurd comedy and want something easy to jump into.
