About Secret Files 2: Puritas Cordis

Secret Files 2: Puritas Cordis is the sequel to the point-and-click adventure Secret Files: Tunguska. The world is suffering famine, floods, economic trouble and wars, and amid these disasters Nina Kalenkov and Max Gruber become tangled in a conspiracy tied to a secretive religious group that seems to know more about the coming crises than it should.

The game is played the classic way: exploring locations, talking to characters and combining items to solve puzzles. It is single-player and unhurried, suited to fans of traditional adventure games and of the first game who want to follow Nina and Max further.
