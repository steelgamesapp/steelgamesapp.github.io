About Chaos Reborn

Julian Gollop, designer of the original XCOM, is behind this turn-based wizard duelling game that mixes chess-like positioning with poker-style bluffing. Between two and six players duel in arenas using a deck of spells and summoned creatures such as the Gooey Blob and Shadow Wood.

Single-player Realm Quests send you up against Kings and Wizard Lords, and you can also bring others into co-op battles or invade another player's realm. Online options include monthly leagues and both live and asynchronous matches. With wizard customization and a large pool of gear, it suits tactics fans who enjoy unpredictability and mind games.
