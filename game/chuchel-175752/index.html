About CHUCHEL

Chuchel is a hairy little creature obsessed with a cherry, and his rival Kekel wants it just as badly. Each scene is a self-contained puzzle or gag that you solve by interacting with the surroundings and with the odd characters nearby, and the screen is full of quirky animation.

Made by Amanita Design, the studio behind Machinarium, Botanicula and Samorost, it leans on situational comedy rather than dialogue, and some stages riff on classic video games. The soundtrack is by the band DVA. Single-player and low on pressure, it suits players who want a silly, charming puzzle adventure.
