About Wreckfest

Wreckfest is a demolition derby themed racing game from Bugbear, the makers of FlatOut. You race and crash through events with soft-body damage that makes every collision feel real.

There are both demolition derbies and more traditional races, with in-depth vehicle upgrades and customization. You can play a career or race online. It suits players who love racing with plenty of destruction.
