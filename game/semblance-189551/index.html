About Semblance

Most platformers treat the ground as fixed. Semblance, set in a minimalist world where nothing is hard, lets you push, squash and reshape the terrain itself. If a collectible is out of reach, you deform and raise a section of the world to get there. Across three worlds, each exploring a different consequence of a shifting planet, you push back a crystalline growth that hardens everything it touches by freeing the life essences.

Nyamakop's game has a calm, minimalist look, full controller support and a single-player focus. It suits puzzle-platformer fans who enjoy experimenting with an unusual core mechanic.
