About Hard West

Warren's life is upended by tragedy, and his quest for revenge drags him into a supernatural version of the Wild West, where he hunts down everyone who wronged him. Dark pacts with powers beyond comprehension carry lasting consequences for the people around him.

Hard West is a turn-based tactics game with eight story-driven scenarios and 40 individually designed combat missions. A varied cast of playable characters have fates tied to yours, and hard choices shape the outcome. It is a single-player game with full controller support, suited to tactics fans who like a Western with an occult streak.
