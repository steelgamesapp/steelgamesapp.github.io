About The Surge

After a catastrophe on your first day at the CREO megacorporation, you wake in a heavy-duty exoskeleton inside a wrecked part of the complex. Malfunctioning robots, deranged augmented co-workers and a rogue AI all want you dead, and you have to fight your way through them.

Combat is close-range and deliberate, and you can target specific limbs on an enemy. Whatever you cut off can drop as loot, which feeds into upgrading and crafting weapons and armour. Deck13 developed this single-player sci-fi action RPG, which has full controller support. It suits players who enjoy challenging melee fights and loot-driven progression.
