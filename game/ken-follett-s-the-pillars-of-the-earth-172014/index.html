About Ken Follett's The Pillars of the Earth

This adventure retells Ken Follett's novel about a cathedral being raised in twelfth-century England. You take turns as Philip, Jack and Aliena, three people whose paths cross in the town of Kingsbridge while hardship and conflict press in on them. Exploring, talking and making decisions lets you alter how events from the book play out.

The pace is unhurried and driven by story, closer to an interactive novel than an action game. Conversation and choices matter more than quick reactions. Readers of the book and fans of narrative adventures are the natural audience. It is a single-player game with full controller support.
