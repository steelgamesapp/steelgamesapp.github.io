About King's Bounty: Armored Princess

Playing Princess Amelie, you lead an army across Teana, searching for a legendary knight named Bill Gilbert. You explore a large hand-built world map, recruit creatures to your side, and fight turn-based battles in which each unit's position and abilities matter.

Each region brings tougher enemies and bigger rewards, with powerful bosses and a new race of Lizards among the foes. The game continues from the earlier King's Bounty: The Legend with deeper tactics, so it suits fans of turn-based strategy and role-playing who like planning army builds and adapting to new threats. It is single-player.
