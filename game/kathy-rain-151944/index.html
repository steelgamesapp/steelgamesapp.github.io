About Kathy Rain

In the 1990s, a determined journalism student named Kathy sets out on her motorcycle to investigate the death of her grandfather, Joseph Rain, which is wrapped in mystery. Armed with a notepad and a pack of cigarettes, she works through the clues he left in Conwell Springs, a town where a local mystery and a string of people going mad point toward something dark.

Kathy Rain is a single-player point-and-click adventure, drawn in low-resolution pixel art in a 4:3 format. The story also forces her to face her troubled past. It suits fans of classic adventure games who like a mystery with a serious tone, plenty of dialogue and puzzle solving.
