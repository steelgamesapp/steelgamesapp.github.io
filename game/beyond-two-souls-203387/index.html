About Beyond: Two Souls

Many years of Jodie Holmes's life, from childhood onward, unfold in this cinematic thriller. Jodie is linked to Aiden, a supernatural entity, and that bond makes her both powerful and an outsider. You guide her through the dangers and losses that come with this connection as she tries to learn who she is, with action sequences, investigation and quiet character moments all playing a part.

The cast is led by Willem Dafoe and Elliot Page. The choices and actions you take influence where Jodie's story goes. Co-op allows a second player to take control of Aiden alongside the first, though the game is fully playable alone. It suits players who value cinematic, character-led storytelling over mechanical complexity.
