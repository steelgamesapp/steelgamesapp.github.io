About Saturnalia

Four characters take turns exploring an isolated Sardinian village shadowed by an age-old ritual in this third-person horror adventure. Each has a distinct ability suited to a different kind of exploration or investigation, and together they uncover an intergenerational story rooted in local folklore.

The village is a maze, and you get little guidance. Along the way you pick up tools, work out puzzles and unlock shortcuts to new areas. When every character has died, the layout reshuffles into a new form, so the map never stays fully familiar. It is single-player with full controller support, aimed at horror fans who like disorientation, self-directed exploration and a story pieced together slowly.
