About Deus Ex: Mankind Divided

Deus Ex: Mankind Divided is an action role-playing game set in 2029, in a world that despises augmented humans. You play Adam Jensen, a veteran covert operative who works for an anti-terrorist task force while unraveling a worldwide conspiracy.

You can approach almost every situation in several ways, using stealth, hacking, conversation or combat, and your augmentations let you build your playstyle. The main hub is a detailed version of Prague full of side missions. It is a single-player game for fans of immersive, choice-driven action.
