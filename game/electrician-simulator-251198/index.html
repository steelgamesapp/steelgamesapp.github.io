About Electrician Simulator

Electrician Simulator puts you in the shoes of an electrician learning the basics of the job. You visit customers' homes to install sockets, replace light bulbs, lay wires, repair worn-out equipment and assemble broken appliances, checking your work carefully as you go.

Jobs get more complex as you progress, and you earn money to buy better tools and take on bigger tasks. The game shows how real wiring works in a hands-on way, and mistakes have consequences. It is a calm, detailed single-player sim for players who like learning practical skills through play.
