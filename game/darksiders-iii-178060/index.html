About Darksiders III

Fury, one of the Four Horsemen, returns to a ruined Earth with a single job: tracking down and defeating the Seven Deadly Sins. She fights in third-person melee with a whip and magic, and her different forms unlock new weapons, moves and ways to get around. The world is open-ended and interconnected, so you keep doubling back through earlier areas as new abilities let you reach places that were closed off.

This is the third main Darksiders game, made by Gunfire Games. It is a single-player hack-and-slash adventure with full controller support, and it suits players who like exploration, combat against large enemies and an apocalyptic fantasy setting.
