About Going Under

Failed tech startups literally sink underground in this satirical action dungeon crawler, and their cursed employees wander the halls as monsters. You play an unpaid intern working in Neo-Cascadia, a dystopian city, whose job is to descend into these ruins, defeat the monsters and repossess the company's assets, using office junk as weapons.

The procedurally generated dungeons are each themed on a type of failed startup, such as gig work, dating sites and cryptocurrency, and the humor in Going Under pokes fun at tech hype. Single-player with full controller support, it suits players who enjoy action-oriented dungeon runs with a comedic voice.
