About Resident Evil 3

Jill Valentine tries to escape Raccoon City in this survival horror game, one of the last people left who saw what Umbrella did there. To silence her, Umbrella sends its secret weapon, Nemesis, a relentless pursuer that stalks her through the ruined streets. You move through the city in third person, fighting infected, solving light puzzles and managing ammunition.

Bundled with it is Resident Evil Resistance, an online asymmetric mode in which four survivors face a Mastermind who controls the environment and its threats. The campaign is single-player, and the online mode is player-versus-player, with full controller support throughout. It suits players who want survival horror with a strong action emphasis.
