About The Book of Unwritten Tales

The Book of Unwritten Tales is a fantasy point-and-click adventure from KING Art. In a world torn by war, an old gremlin archaeologist named Mortimer MacGuffin holds the secret of a powerful artifact.

You play several heroes who team up to find it, including a gnome and an elf princess, solving puzzles and talking to odd characters. The game is full of humor and parodies of fantasy stories. It suits fans of classic adventures with a lighthearted tone.
