About Deponia Doomsday

Rufus wakes from a nightmare in which he saves Deponia by sacrificing himself and ends up with a mustache. Convinced these events must never happen, he teams up with McChronicle, an eccentric scientist who studies time anomalies, and discovers that visitors from the future have left a time machine lying around near his home.

Deponia Doomsday is a comedy point-and-click adventure from Daedalic Entertainment, and a later entry in the Deponia series, where Rufus tries to change the planet's fate through time travel without destroying it. Jokes and puzzle chains drive the experience. It is single-player, and it suits adventure fans who enjoy absurd humour and inventory puzzles.
