Press and partners

SteelGames is a veteran-owned PC game deals site and short-form video channel. We help players find real deals from official stores, set up their games and gear, and avoid fake keys.

What we cover

  • Game deals: sales from official stores, checked against price history, with a deal rating on every game page.
  • Settings and how-to Shorts: best settings for PC, PS5, Xbox, Switch 2 and the ROG Xbox Ally X, performance fixes and Steam tips.
  • Gear: mice, keyboards, headsets, chairs and prebuilt PCs, matched to the games they suit.
  • Veteran and first responder discounts across gaming and PC hardware.

Where your game shows up

YouTube Shorts: new Shorts every day, also posted to TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook.

steelgamesapp.com: hundreds of game pages with descriptions, screenshots, price history and works-best-on info.

SteelGames Discord: daily deal posts and community polls.

Steam Curator: recommendations on our SteelGames Deals curator page.

What we do with review keys

  • A Short on the game: first look, best settings, or whether it's worth it at its price.
  • A write-up on the game's page on steelgamesapp.com.
  • A post in the SteelGames Discord.

We send you links to all coverage. Review copies are always disclosed, and a free copy never buys a positive opinion.

Audience

PC and console players looking to save money, in the US and around the world. Current channel and site stats are available on request.
