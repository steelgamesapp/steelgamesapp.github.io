Buying games

Official store or key reseller?

When you buy from Steam, Epic or GOG, the publisher gets paid and the store handles refunds. A key reseller sells an activation key it got somewhere else. You still play the game on the same launcher, and it's often cheaper.

  • Check the key's region before you buy.
  • Read the reseller's refund policy, since the official store can't help with a reseller key.
  • Be careful with open marketplaces, where anyone can list keys.

Deals

Is this sale actually good?

A big percentage off doesn't always mean a low price. Some games go on sale every few weeks, and some list prices change before a sale starts.

  • Compare today's price with the game's lowest price in the past year.
  • Watch for seasonal sales, which often bring the deepest cuts.
  • Set a price alert instead of buying at the first discount.

Setup

Building your first gaming desk

Spend first on what you touch and sit in every day. A good chair and a monitor at eye level make long sessions more comfortable than any amount of RGB.

  • Chair and desk height first, then the monitor.
  • A large mousepad gives you room for low-sensitivity aim.
  • Add lighting and a stream deck once the basics feel right.

Help

Common questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

Does SteelGames sell games?

No. We show you where a game is cheapest and send you to that store. You pay the store directly, the same way you normally would.

Is it free?

Yes. The website, the app and the Discord server are all free.

How does SteelGames make money?

Some store links pay us a small commission when you buy, at no extra cost to you. Every link is labeled, and the free app shows ads.

What is a "resold key"?

A code for a game sold by a third-party site instead of the official store. It's often cheaper, but refunds go through that site, not the official store. We always label these.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse prices and gear without one. An account lets you set price alerts, earn points, write reviews and chat.

How do I delete my account?

To permanently delete your account, open the app and navigate to Account > Delete Account. Your profile, posts, and points will be permanently removed.

I need help from a person.

Email us at steelgames.app@outlook.com or ask in the #app-help channel on our Discord. We read every message.
