Buying games

Official store or key reseller?

When you buy from Steam, Epic or GOG, the publisher gets paid and the store handles refunds. A key reseller sells an activation key it got somewhere else. You still play the game on the same launcher, and it's often cheaper.

  • Check the key's region before you buy.
  • Read the reseller's refund policy, since the official store can't help with a reseller key.
  • Be careful with open marketplaces, where anyone can list keys.

Deals

Is this sale actually good?

A big percentage off doesn't always mean a low price. Some games go on sale every few weeks, and some list prices change before a sale starts.

  • Compare today's price with the game's lowest price in the past year.
  • Watch for seasonal sales, which often bring the deepest cuts.
  • Set a price alert instead of buying at the first discount.

Setup

Building your first gaming desk

Spend first on what you touch and sit in every day. A good chair and a monitor at eye level make long sessions more comfortable than any amount of RGB.

  • Chair and desk height first, then the monitor.
  • A large mousepad gives you room for low-sensitivity aim.
  • Add lighting and a stream deck once the basics feel right.

Privacy policy

Your data and Steel Games

Last updated October 26, 2026.

What we collect

You can browse Steel Games without an account. We never ask for or store payment details.

Steel Games accounts

If you create an account, we store your email address, your username and what you post. Reviews, chat messages and comments are public under your username. If you upload a receipt to become a Verified Buyer, only our moderators can see it. You can delete your posts at any time, and you can delete your whole account in the app under Account → Delete account, which permanently removes your profile, posts, reviews, receipts and points.

Links to stores and retailers

Some links on Steel Games are affiliate links. When you follow one, the store or retailer may use cookies to record that you came from Steel Games so we can earn a commission if you make a purchase. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave Steel Games.

The Steel Games app

  • Purchases happen on the store's or retailer's own website. Steel Games never sees your card or payment details.
  • Price alerts and wishlists are used only to send you the notifications you set up.
  • The free version shows ads, and ad providers may use device identifiers as described in their own policies.
