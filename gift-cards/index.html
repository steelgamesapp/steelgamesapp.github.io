Steam Wallet and Fortnite V-Bucks

When we checked resale sites, Steam Wallet cards and V-Bucks cost more than buying them directly, so we don't list them. Add Steam Wallet funds in the Steam app or buy a card at a store near you, and buy V-Bucks inside Fortnite. If you buy V-Bucks on Xbox or PlayStation, an Xbox or PlayStation card above pays for them.

Before you buy a gift card code

  • Check the region. A US card only works on a US account.
  • Compare the price at checkout, fees included, with the face value. If it's higher, buy from the console's own store instead.
  • Pick a seller with lots of good ratings, and redeem the code as soon as it arrives.
  • Skip the add-ons at checkout unless you want them. They raise the price.
