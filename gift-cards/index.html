Before you buy a gift card code

  • Check the region. A US card only works on a US account.
  • Prices marked ≈ were converted to dollars from the store's feed. Stores can add a payment or service fee at checkout, so compare the final total with the face value. If it's higher, buy from the official store instead.
  • Kinguin is a marketplace: pick a seller with lots of good ratings, and redeem the code as soon as it arrives.
  • Skip the add-ons at checkout unless you want them. They raise the price.
