Get GTA 6

Every boxed copy is a download code, not a disc, so the box and the digital store version are the same game. PS5 codes are for the region on the box, so buy the US version for a US account.

Make room for GTA 6

GTA 6 is a very big download. Check your free space in Settings > Storage before it fails halfway. On PS5, any fast M.2 SSD with a heatsink goes in the expansion slot. On Xbox Series X|S, new games have to run from the internal drive or an official expansion card; a USB drive can only store them.

TVs

For PS5 Pro and Series X, look for HDMI 2.1, 120Hz and good HDR. These three cover the price range.

Gaming monitors

PS5 and Xbox Series X|S output 4K or 1440p to a monitor over HDMI. A 4K monitor with HDMI 2.1 gets you 120Hz at 4K.

Headsets

Wired 3.5mm headsets plug into any PS5 or Xbox controller. Wireless ones need the right version for your console.

Consoles

Since GTA 6 is a download on every platform, a digital console (no disc drive) plays it the same way.

More GTA and Rockstar games

Waiting for your download, or want the back catalog? These play on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (the PS4 and Xbox One versions run on the newer consoles too).

For GTA Online (in GTA V)

GTA 6 launched as a single-player game, and Rockstar hasn't given a date for its online mode, so you don't need a membership to play GTA 6 right now. You do need PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass Essential to play GTA Online in GTA V on a console.
