Steam Winter Sale 2026: Dates and How to Spot a Real Discount

The Steam Winter Sale 2026 runs from December 17, 2026 to January 4, 2027. Big red percent-off badges show up everywhere, but not every discount is a good price. Here is how to plan, and how to tell a real sale from a fake one.

Why a big percent off can still be a bad deal

A badge that says 75% off only tells you the gap between a list price and today's price. It says nothing about whether today's price is low. Three things to watch for:

  • Old games always look big. A ten-year-old game that sits at a low price all year may be marked 80% off for a sale, but the price you pay is the same one you could have paid in July.
  • Sale prices repeat. Most games return to the same discount over and over. If a game has hit this price before, you lose nothing by waiting for the next sale.
  • Bundles hide the real cost. A bundle can be a bargain, or it can pad the price with DLC and extras you do not want. Compare the bundle against the games you would actually play.

A five-step check for any PC game sale

  1. Ignore the percent. Read the price. Decide what the game is worth to you in hours of play, then compare.
  2. Check the price history. On SteelGames, each game's price page shows how low the price has gone. If today's price matches the lowest on record, it is a real deal. If it is above, expect it to drop again.
  3. Compare official stores. The same game can be cheaper on Epic or GOG. See our Epic Games Store deals, GOG deals and best Steam deals today lists.
  4. Check what you are buying. Make sure it is the base game and not a bundle, a DLC pack or a different edition.
  5. Use your wishlist. Wishlist the games you want now, and buy only the ones that hit a price you are happy with.

If you have a short list, you can shop Black Friday weekend and then check again in the Winter Sale. Games you have waited on for a year are usually the best buys. Games you only just heard about are usually not urgent.

Keys from resellers: a separate decision

Official stores like Steam, Epic and GOG sell directly and are the safest place to buy. Key resellers sometimes list the same game for less, but they are a different kind of seller. Questions

When is the Steam Winter Sale 2026?

Steam's Winter Sale 2026 runs from December 17, 2026 to January 4, 2027, starting at 10am Pacific. Dates checked on November 5, 2026 against Valve's published Steamworks calendar as reported by several outlets.

Is Black Friday better than the Steam Winter Sale for PC games?

Neither always wins. Black Friday weekend (November 27 to 30, 2026) brings deals across many stores, while the Winter Sale is Steam's longest sale. Compare the same game on both before you decide.

How do I know a PC game discount is real?

Look at the price you would actually pay, not the percent off. Check the game's price history and compare official stores such as Steam, Epic and GOG. If the sale price has been seen before, it is a normal price, not a special one.

Should I wait for a lower price?

If the game is not on your must-play list, waiting is fine. If it is at or near its lowest recorded price and you will play it soon, buy it. A wishlist alert costs nothing.
