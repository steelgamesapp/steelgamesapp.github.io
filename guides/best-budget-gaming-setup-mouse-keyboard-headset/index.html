Questions

Do I need a mechanical keyboard to game?

No. A mechanical board feels better to type on and lasts longer, but a basic keyboard works. If you want the cheapest start, the keyboard and mouse combo covers both.

Wireless or wired?

Wired is cheaper and has no battery to charge. Modern wireless mice like the G305 are fast enough for nearly everyone.
