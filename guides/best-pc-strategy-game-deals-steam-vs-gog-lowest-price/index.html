It depends on the game and the day. On November 13, 2026 the lowest official price was on GOG for XCOM 2, Against the Storm and Sid Meier's Pirates!, and on Steam for Civilization V, Civilization IV and Company of Heroes. Check each game's price page before you buy.

Not always. Some are at or near their lowest tracked price and some have been cheaper. Each game's SteelGames price page shows the lowest price we track so you can judge it yourself.

Will these strategy game prices last?

Not necessarily. Stores can change prices at any time, and Black Friday is November 27, 2026, so some of these may drop again. Prices checked November 13, 2026.
