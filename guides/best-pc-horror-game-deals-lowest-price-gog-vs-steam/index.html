Why GOG wins this list

All eight games are cheapest on GOG today, usually by a wide margin. Alan Wake is $1.49 and The Evil Within is $4.99. GOG sells DRM-free installers you can keep without a launcher, which suits older horror games well. Steam still has the larger library and Workshop mods, so see our guide to Steam vs Epic vs GOG before you commit to one store.

Best picks by kind of scare

  • Hide and run: Outlast and Amnesia: The Dark Descent. You have no weapons, so every corridor is a risk.
  • Story and dread: SOMA and Alan Wake. Less jump-scare, more unease. Alan Wake is the cheapest buy on the list.
  • Survival horror: Tormented Souls and The Evil Within. Limited ammo, tough fights, old-school pacing. More at survival game deals.
  • One big stealth game: Alien: Isolation at $11.99 is the priciest pick, and the one most worth waiting on if you can.
