Buy now or wait for Black Friday?

Black Friday falls on November 27, 2026, and the Steam Winter Sale is scheduled for December 17, 2026 to January 4, 2027. Valve has not published exact Black Friday discount dates, so nothing is guaranteed. A simple rule: if the price is within a few dollars of the lowest tracked, buy. If it is more than double, wait. Questions

Which survival games are on sale right now?

On November 18, 2026 the official-store sales we found were Frostpunk at $4.49 on GOG, Satisfactory at $27.99 on the Epic Games Store and No Man's Sky at $23.99 on GOG. Subnautica, Valheim, Raft, The Forest and Project Zomboid were at regular price.

Should I wait for Black Friday or the Steam Winter Sale?

For games already near their lowest tracked price, buy now. For games at full price, waiting usually pays: Black Friday falls on November 27, 2026 and the Steam Winter Sale runs December 17, 2026 to January 4, 2027. Valve has not published exact Black Friday discount dates, and no discount is guaranteed.

How do I know a survival game price is good?

Open the game's SteelGames price page and compare today's price to the lowest price we track. If it is far above that number, waiting is reasonable.

Is GOG or Steam cheaper for survival games?

It changes by game. This week GOG had Frostpunk and No Man's Sky cheapest, while Epic had Satisfactory cheapest. Steam had none of these three discounted.
