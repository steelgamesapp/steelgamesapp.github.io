Is Humble Choice Worth It? Bundle vs Buying on Sale (November 2026)

Humble Choice for November 2026 packs eight PC games into one $14.99 price. That sounds like a steal, but only if you would actually play them. Here is a plain way to check, using real prices.

What is in Humble Choice this month?

The November 2026 lineup is Mafia: The Old Country (with two DLC packs), The Outer Worlds 2, SHINOBI: Art of Vengeance, Yakuza 6: The Song of Life, Crisol: Theater of Idols, Space Haven, DYSMANTLE and Squeakross: Home Squeak Home. The break-even math

At $14.99, the bundle beats buying separately as soon as your picks cost more than that. Yakuza 6 ($9.29) plus Space Haven ($14.99) already tops it. Add SHINOBI ($17.99) and you are well ahead. Pick The Outer Worlds 2 or Mafia, and the bundle wins on those two alone.

  • Want 3 or more games: the bundle is very likely the cheaper route.
  • Want 1 or 2 cheap games: DYSMANTLE at $6.99 or Yakuza 6 at $9.29 costs less on its own.
  • Already own several: count only the games you would actually claim and play.

When buying on sale wins

A bundle is only a deal if the games are ones you want. A $15 charge for games you never launch is $15 wasted. If your wishlist has one or two titles, wait for them to hit a low price instead. Bundle checklist before you pay

  1. Count the games you would really play. Skip the rest.
  2. Check the store. Know where keys redeem (Steam, Battle.net or elsewhere).
  3. Compare to the lowest price. Use the price pages above.
  4. Read the terms. Look at what you keep if you cancel, directly on Humble's page.
