Questions

Is Steam or GOG cheaper for RPGs?

It depends on the game and the day. On November 17, 2026 GOG was the lone cheapest official store for Pillars of Eternity II, Dragon's Dogma: Dark Arisen and Disco Elysium - The Final Cut, Steam was cheapest for Persona 4 Golden, and several games tied. Check each game's price page before you buy.

Are these the lowest prices ever for these RPGs?

Not always. Some are at or near their lowest tracked price and some have been cheaper. Each game's SteelGames price page shows the lowest price we track so you can judge it yourself.

Will these RPG prices last?

Not necessarily. Stores can change prices at any time, and Black Friday is November 27, 2026, so some of these may drop again. Prices checked November 17, 2026.

Can I use mods if I buy on GOG?

Most big RPG mods work on either store's version, but Steam has built-in Workshop support for some games. Check the mod page for the store version it supports before you buy.
