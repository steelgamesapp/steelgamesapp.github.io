Start with a wishlist, not the sale

The best Black Friday habit is to decide what you want before the banners appear. List the games you have been waiting on. Games you have wanted for a year are usually the best buys. Games you only just heard about can wait for the Winter Sale or later.

How to spot a fake Black Friday discount

  1. Ignore the percent. Read the price. A big badge only compares against a list price.
  2. Check the price history. If today's price matches the lowest on record, it is a real deal. If not, it will probably drop again.
  3. Know what you are buying. Make sure it is the base game, not a bundle or a pricier edition.
  4. Do not rush. Black Friday is followed by Cyber Monday and then the Winter Sale. Our guide to the Steam Winter Sale 2026 and how to spot a real discount covers the same checks in more detail.

Questions

When is Black Friday 2026 for PC games?

Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. Many stores start early, so check deals in the days before. Dates checked on November 5, 2026.

When is the Epic Games Store Black Friday sale in 2026?

Epic's own Q4 2026 promotions calendar lists a Black Friday sale from November 17 to December 1, 2026, but it covers Premium Editions only (Deluxe, Gold, Premium and similar versions). Base games and add-ons are in Epic's Holiday Sale, December 10, 2026 to January 7, 2027. Dates checked on November 5, 2026.

Is Black Friday the best time to buy PC games?

It is one of the best, along with the Steam Winter Sale in December. Compare the same game across official stores and check its price history before you buy.

Should I buy game keys from resellers on Black Friday?

Official stores are the safest choice. Resellers can be cheaper, but read their refund and region terms first, and make sure the key works on the store you use.

How do I stop overspending on Black Friday?

Make a wishlist now, set a budget, and buy only games that are at or near their lowest recorded price.
