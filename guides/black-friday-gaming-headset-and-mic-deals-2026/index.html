How to tell if a Black Friday gear deal is real

Black Friday is a good time to buy gaming gear, but not every red "deal" tag is a real drop. A few quick checks help.

  • Look at the price history. A browser extension or price tracker shows whether today's price is really lower than the last few months, or only lower than an inflated list price.
  • Check the exact model. Many products come in wired and wireless versions, or in several colors at different prices. Make sure the listing is the version you want.
  • Buy from Amazon or the brand's own store. On the product page, check that it says "Ships from Amazon" or "Sold by" the brand, so returns and warranty are simple.
  • Keep the box. Holiday purchases on Amazon usually get a longer return window, so you have time to make sure it fits.

Questions

Do I need a separate mic if my headset has one?

No. A headset mic is fine for games and Discord. A USB mic like the QuadCast 2 sounds clearer and fuller, which matters most if you stream or record.

Wired or wireless headset?

Wired is cheaper and never needs charging. Wireless lets you move around and keeps cables off the desk, but you have to charge it.

When does Amazon Black Friday end?

Amazon says its Black Friday Week runs November 19 to 30, 2026. Individual deals can end sooner or sell out.
