Questions

1080p or 1440p?

1440p is noticeably sharper on a 27-inch screen. On a 24-inch screen 1080p still looks fine and runs on weaker graphics cards.

Does a higher refresh rate help?

Yes, but only if your PC can produce the frames. A 144 to 180 Hz panel is plenty for most budget builds.
