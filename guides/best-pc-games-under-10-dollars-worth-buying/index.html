Why each one made the list

  • Disco Elysium - The Final Cut ($7.89 on IndieGala). A landmark detective RPG that is almost all dialogue and choices. At $7.89 for the Final Cut it is the priciest pick here and still a strong value.
  • Metro Exodus ($2.99 on GOG). A story-driven shooter that mixes tight corridors with open hub levels. DRM-free on GOG.
  • Trine 4: The Nightmare Prince ($2.99 on IndieGala). A beautiful physics-puzzle platformer you can play solo or in co-op.
  • Sid Meier's Civilization IV ($3.99 on Steam). The turn-based strategy classic. Old graphics, deep systems, easy on any PC.
  • Company of Heroes ($2.99 on Steam). A WWII real-time strategy game that still sets the bar for squad tactics.
  • SteamWorld Dig 2 ($1.99 on GOG). A polished digging platformer with great pacing. Overwhelmingly positive reviews.
  • Strange Brigade - Deluxe Edition ($6.40 on Green Man Gaming). A co-op third-person shooter in a pulp-adventure setting. Best with friends. The Deluxe Edition includes extra content.
  • Sniper Elite 4 Deluxe Edition ($7.20 on Green Man Gaming). Slow, tactical sniping across big Italian maps. The kill-cam is the draw. The Deluxe Edition adds bonus content.

Is a game under $10 actually worth it?

Price alone is not value. Cost per hour of fun is. A $7 game you finish in 40 hours beats a $2 game you never open. Use these checks:

  1. Read the reviews. We lean toward 85% positive and up on Steam. Mixed reviews at a low price are usually mixed for a reason.
  2. Check the price history. If today's price matches the lowest on record, it is a real deal. Our guide on how to tell a real PC game sale from a fake one walks through it.
  3. Watch for editions. Deluxe and Ultimate editions can look cheap but add bundles of cosmetics. Make sure you are paying for what you want.
  4. Check your hardware. Older games run on almost anything. Newer ones like Metro Exodus want a decent graphics card, so read the system requirements.
