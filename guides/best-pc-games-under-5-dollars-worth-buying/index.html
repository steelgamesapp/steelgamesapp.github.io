Why each one made the list

  • Hitman: Blood Money ($0.99 on Steam). A classic stealth sandbox. It is an older game, so expect dated controls, but the level design still holds up.
  • Lacuna: A Sci-Fi Noir Adventure ($1.59 on Steam). A short, story-driven detective game. Good for a weekend.
  • Borderlands 2 ($1.99 on Steam). Co-op looter-shooter with a huge amount of content for the price.
  • This War of Mine ($1.59 on Fanatical, $1.99 on Steam). A survival game about civilians in a besieged city. Heavy, but well regarded.
  • Owlboy ($1.99 on Humble Store). A hand-drawn platformer with a strong story. Humble is an official store.
  • Roki ($1.99 on Steam). A Scandinavian-folklore puzzle adventure with a gentle pace.
  • Frostpunk ($2.39 on Fanatical, $2.99 on Steam). A city-survival builder where every decision is a hard one. Great value even at this price.
  • Dropsy ($0.99 on Steam). A quirky point-and-click adventure with no dialogue. Short and memorable.

How to pick a cheap game you will actually play

  1. Look at reviews first. A low price does not fix a game that players dislike. We favor 85% positive and up.
  2. Check the price history. A game at its lowest recorded price is a real deal. See our guide on how to tell a real PC game sale from a fake one.
  3. Match the game to your PC. Older games run on almost anything, but check the system requirements on the store page.
  4. Be honest about your backlog. A $2 game you never launch is not a bargain. Buy what you will play this month.

Where to buy: official stores first

Buy from an official store when you can. Steam, GOG, Humble, Fanatical and the Epic Games Store all run deep discounts on older games.
