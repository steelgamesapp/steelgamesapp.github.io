Best wireless keyboard gift

SOLAKAKA A75 Pro

Wireless 75% mechanical keyboard
$59.99at SOLAKAKA. Store sale price; regular $79.99 when we checked.

A compact 75% keyboard that still keeps the arrow keys and the F-key row, so it fits a small desk without losing the keys PC games use. It connects three ways: 2.4GHz wireless, Bluetooth 5.0 or a USB-C cable.

  • 4,000mAh rechargeable battery
  • Built-in LCD screen and a CNC rotary knob

Best gift for competitive players

EPOMAKER HE80

Wired 75% magnetic-switch keyboard
$59.49at EPOMAKER. Store sale price; regular $69.99 when we checked.

Magnetic Hall effect switches let you set how far each key travels before it fires, in 0.02mm steps, and Fast Trigger resets a key the moment you lift it. Snap Key (SOCD) and 8000Hz polling are features competitive players look for.

  • 75% layout with 82 lubed keys
  • EPOMAKER MagneX magnetic switches with 0.02mm Fast Trigger
  • 8000Hz polling rate
  • Double-shot PBT keycaps and south-facing RGB; wired only
Shop at EPOMAKER
Best MMO mouse gift

Redragon Impact Elite M913

Wireless MMO mouse with 12 side buttons
From $46.99at Redragon. $46.99 for the 2.4GHz version, $52.99 for the tri-mode version with Bluetooth.

Sixteen programmable buttons, twelve of them on the side, put MMO and MOBA hotkeys under the thumb. It uses a PixArt 3395 sensor and comes in a 2.4GHz version or a tri-mode version that adds Bluetooth.

  • 16 programmable buttons, including 12 side buttons
  • PixArt 3395 sensor, up to 26,000 DPI in the software
  • Battery up to 250 hours (tri-mode version, RGB off)
  • Up to 45 hours on the 2.4GHz version (RGB off)
Shop at Redragon
