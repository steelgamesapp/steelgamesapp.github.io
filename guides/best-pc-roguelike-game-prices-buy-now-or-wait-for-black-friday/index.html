Which roguelikes are worth buying right now

Only a few games on this list are on sale today. Noita is $7.99 on GOG and sits at its lowest tracked price, so there is little reason to wait. Children of Morta at $3.29 is close to its low of $2.19, and Vampire Survivors at $4.99 is an easy pick if you want a cheap, hands-on game tonight. See more in our best PC games under $5 guide.

Which ones to wait on

Hades, Slay the Spire and Dead Cells are all at $24.99 on Steam today. Each has sold for far less before: Slay the Spire as low as $2.49, Hades at $6.24 and Dead Cells at $9.26. Enter the Gungeon and The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth are $14.99 and have both been as low as $1.49. If you can wait a few weeks, these are the ones to put on a wishlist. Steam, GOG or Epic for roguelikes?

Roguelikes are mostly single-player and run well on modest PCs, so the cheapest store is usually the best store. GOG is lowest on Noita, Cult of the Lamb, Children of Morta and Enter the Gungeon today. Steam wins on the big full-price names and has the largest library, plus cloud saves on most titles.
