How to Tell a Real PC Game Sale from a Fake One

A red "-75%" badge feels like a win, but it only tells you how far the price fell from the list price. It does not tell you whether the price is actually low. Here are five quick checks to separate a real PC game sale from a fake one before you spend a dollar.

What makes a sale "fake"?

A fake sale is rarely a lie about the price. It is a true price presented in a misleading way. The usual tricks: a list price set high so the percent off looks huge, a "limited time" label on a price that comes back every few weeks, or a "sale" that is no cheaper than last month. The price you pay is real. The savings are not.

Check 1: Compare to the lowest price on record

This is the one check that matters most. Open the game's price page from our games list and look at the lowest price we track. If today's price matches it, buy with confidence. If today's price is far above it, you are looking at a discount on paper only. Our guide to tracking PC game prices with wishlists and price history shows the full routine.

Check 2: Ignore the percent off

Percent off is measured against the list price, and the list price is whatever the publisher says it is. A 50 percent cut from a high price can still cost more than a 30 percent cut from a lower one. Compare the final dollar amount, not the badge.

Check 3: Know that sales repeat

Publishers cycle the same discounts through seasonal events. Steam's 2026 Autumn Sale already ran October 1 to 8, and the Steam Winter Sale is scheduled for December 17, 2026 to January 4, 2027. Black Friday is November 27 and Cyber Monday is November 30. If you miss a deal, it often returns. Read Steam Winter Sale 2026: how to spot a real discount to plan around it.

Check 4: Compare official stores

The same game can have different prices on Steam, the Epic Games Store and GOG on the same day. Check all of them before you buy, and remember that your library lives on the store you choose. We break down the trade-offs in Steam vs Epic vs GOG: where to buy PC games cheapest. Each store has its own deal list: Best Steam deals today, Epic Games Store deals and GOG game deals.

Check 5: Know the refund rules

If you do get burned, a refund is your safety net. Valve's published Steam policy is generally 14 days from purchase and under 2 hours of play, with exceptions, so confirm the current terms on Valve's refund page. Other stores have their own rules. Look them up before you buy, not after.

Questions

How can I tell if a PC game sale is real?

Check the lowest price we track on the game's SteelGames price page, then compare today's price to it. If today's price is at or near the lowest on record, the sale is real. If it is well above, it is a discount on paper only. Checked November 4, 2026.

Does a big percent-off number mean a good deal?

No. Percent off is measured from the list price, and a high list price makes any discount look big. Judge the actual price against the game's price history and against other official stores.

Do Steam sales ever repeat the same discount?

Yes. Many games return to the same sale price at each seasonal event, so a discount you missed often comes back. That is why a buy-or-wait rule based on price history works.

Can I get my money back if I buy a bad deal on Steam?

Valve's published policy is generally refunds within 14 days of purchase and under 2 hours of play, with exceptions. Confirm the current terms on Valve's refund page before you rely on it. Checked November 4, 2026.
