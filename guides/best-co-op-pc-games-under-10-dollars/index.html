Why each co-op game made the list

  • Unrailed! ($2.99 at Humble Store). A tiny, loud co-op game where each player grabs wood, iron or water and keeps a train ahead of the rails. It is easy to teach, so it works for mixed skill groups.
  • Content Warning ($3.99 at Fanatical). A group horror game: you explore spooky locations, film what you find and try to get paid. Best with three or four friends and a headset.
  • Vermintide 2 (Warhammer: Vermintide 2) ($4.79 at Fanatical). A four-player co-op action game about fighting through hordes. It is more serious than the others here and rewards a group that sticks together.
  • Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes ($4.94 at Humble Store). One player sees the bomb, the others hold the manual. No one can see the other's screen, so you must talk. Great for a call with friends who do not play many games.
  • Moving Out ($4.99 at Steam). You and up to three friends haul furniture out of houses as fast as possible. Short levels, lots of laughing, low skill bar.
  • Overcooked! 2 ($8.49 at Steam). The kitchen co-op sequel is the priciest on this list, and still under $10. Local and online play for up to four, with a lot of arguing about who dropped the onions.

Co-op classics that are not on sale today

Three famous co-op games sit at $9.99 on Steam today, which is their regular price and not a discount: Portal 2, Left 4 Dead 2 and Terraria. Our tracker has each of them far lower in the past, so if you are not in a hurry, put them on a wishlist and wait for a sale.

How to pick a co-op game your group will finish

  1. Match the group's skill. Party games like Overcooked! 2 and Moving Out suit mixed groups. Vermintide 2 suits people who like to practice.
  2. Check who has to buy it. Some games need a copy per player. Others, like Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes, let friends join with a free manual. Read the store page first.
  3. Count the players. Most of these support four. Make sure it matches your usual group size.

Questions

What are the best co-op PC games under $10?

On November 10, 2026 our tracker showed Unrailed!, Content Warning, Warhammer: Vermintide 2, Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes, Moving Out and Overcooked! 2 all under $10 at official stores. Prices change, so check each price page before you buy.

Do all players need to buy the game?

Usually yes for online co-op, but not always. Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes needs only the bomb player to own the game, since everyone else uses the free manual. Overcooked! 2 and Moving Out also support local play on one PC. Check the store page for each game's rules.

Is a co-op game at full price under $10 a deal?

Not necessarily. Portal 2, Left 4 Dead 2 and Terraria are $9.99 at their regular price, and each has been much cheaper before. Check the price history and wait for a sale if you can.
