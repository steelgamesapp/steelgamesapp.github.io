Should You Pre-Order PC Games or Wait for a Sale?

Pre-ordering feels like locking in a deal, but on PC it rarely is. Stores let you cancel, prices usually drop later, and reviews land on launch day. Here is a plain rule for when to pre-order a PC game and when to wait for the discount.

Why pre-ordering on PC is different

On consoles you used to lock in a disc. On PC you buy a license that stays in your library, and you can usually get the same game on launch day with no reservation. That means a pre-order gives you almost nothing a day-one purchase does not, apart from a possible bonus and a possible early unlock. It costs you something real, though: your money is tied up, and you buy before any reviews exist.

The refund rules: can you cancel?

Good news: the major PC stores are friendlier than you might expect. As publicly reported, Steam lets you refund a pre-purchase any time before release, and the usual 14-day, under-2-hours-played window starts at launch. The Epic Games Store works the same way: cancel before release for a refund, then the 14-day, 2-hour rule applies. GOG advertises a 30-day refund window, but we could not confirm how it treats pre-orders, so read its terms first. Confirm the current wording on Valve's refund page before you rely on it. When waiting for a sale wins

  • Most single-player games. Many titles get a first discount within months and bigger ones at seasonal events. See our Steam Winter Sale 2026 guide and the Black Friday 2026 guide for the calendar.
  • Anything with a pre-order bonus that is only cosmetic. Skins and soundtracks are not worth full price.
  • Games from studios with a rocky launch history. Bugs and performance patches often arrive in the first weeks, and a patched game at a discount beats a buggy one at list price.
  • Games you will not play right away. If your backlog is full, a pre-order just moves the money earlier.

When pre-ordering makes sense

  • Multiplayer games with a launch-week community where your friends will all start together.
  • Limited-run physical items or editions that sell out and are not sold again.
  • A store that gives a real launch discount. Some launches carry a short intro price. Compare it to the price history before you assume it is good.
  • A studio you already trust, where you would buy on day one anyway and the store lets you cancel.

Questions

Can I get a refund on a pre-ordered PC game?

Yes on Steam and the Epic Games Store: both let you cancel a pre-order before release, then apply their usual refund window after launch. GOG's pre-order terms are not clear from public sources, so read GOG's refund page before you pay. Checked November 7, 2026.

Do PC games get cheaper after launch?

Often, but not always. Many games fall in price during seasonal sales, while some hold their price for months. The game's price history on SteelGames shows how it behaved so far.

Is a pre-order bonus worth paying full price?

Usually not. Bonuses are typically cosmetic. Unless you were going to buy at launch anyway, waiting for a real discount saves more than a cosmetic skin is worth.

What is the safest way to buy a new PC game?

Wait for reviews, then buy from an official store with a refund policy you understand. If you pre-order, do it from a store that lets you cancel before release.
