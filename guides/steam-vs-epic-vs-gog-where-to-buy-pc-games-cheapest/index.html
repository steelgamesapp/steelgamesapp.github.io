Steam vs Epic vs GOG: Where to Buy PC Games Cheapest

Steam, the Epic Games Store and GOG all sell the same PC games, but not at the same price and not with the same rules. Here is a plain comparison so you can pick the right store for each purchase and stop paying more than you have to.

The short answer

No single store is cheapest all the time. A game can be lowest on Steam this week, on GOG next week and on Epic during a promotion. The only reliable method is to compare the same game across official stores on the day you buy. That is what the SteelGames price pages are for: each one shows the lowest price we track for a game and links to the store.

Steam: the biggest catalog and the biggest sales

Steam has the widest selection and the most active sale calendar. If a game is on sale anywhere, it is often on Steam too. The trade-off is that you are buying into one client, and discounts on new releases tend to be small. See what is live on our best Steam deals today list, and plan around the big events in our guide to the Steam Winter Sale 2026.

Epic Games Store: free games and promotions

Epic is worth a launcher install mostly for its free game giveaways and its own promotions. Free beats any discount, so keep an eye on our free PC games right now list. For paid games, browse Epic Games Store deals and compare the price against Steam before you commit.

GOG: DRM-free and a longer refund window

GOG sells DRM-free games, so what you buy keeps working without a launcher running. Its 30-day refund window, even after you have played, is the most forgiving of the three. That makes GOG a low-risk place to try an older or niche title. Browse GOG game deals for what is cheap now.

Bottom line

Use Steam for selection and sales, Epic for freebies, and GOG when you want DRM-free games and a generous refund window. Always compare first. SteelGames is veteran-owned and operated, and we keep it simple: the best PC game deals, with the lowest price per game.

Questions

Is Steam, Epic or GOG cheapest for PC games?

None of them is always cheapest. The same game can cost different amounts at each store on any given day, so compare before you buy. Our price pages show the lowest price we track for each game. Checked November 2, 2026.

Which store has the best refund policy?

GOG offers refunds within 30 days even if you have played the game. Steam and Epic both use a 14-day window with under two hours of playtime. Check each store's current refund page before you buy.

Does GOG really have no DRM?

GOG sells games DRM-free, which means you can install them without a launcher running. Some titles still have online features that need a connection.

Should I use more than one launcher?

Yes, if it saves money. Keys and libraries are tied to each store, so decide where you want each game to live. A single launcher is simpler; several can be cheaper.
