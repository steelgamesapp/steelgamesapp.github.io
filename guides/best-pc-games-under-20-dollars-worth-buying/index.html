Is a game under $20 actually worth it?

A $20 game you finish and enjoy beats a $5 game that sits in your library. Before you buy, run through three checks:

  1. Read the reviews. We lean toward 80% positive and up on Steam, and look at how many reviews there are, not just the percentage.
  2. Compare to the lowest price. Some of these games have been cheaper before, and will be again. Our guide on how to tell a real PC game sale from a fake one shows how to check, and how to track PC game prices shows how to set up a wishlist.
  3. Match it to your hardware. Read the system requirements on the store page before you buy a newer game.
