Monitor

MSI PRO MP243L 24-inch 144Hz Monitor

24-inch 1080p, 144Hz IPS

PC Gamer lists it as a budget 1080p pick: a 24-inch IPS panel with a 144 Hz refresh rate and 1 ms response. It is sold as an all-round monitor, not a gaming one, but the specs are there.

Good: 144 Hz on a budget; IPS colors.
Watch for: Small; basic stand.

Keyboard

RK ROYAL KLUDGE R65 Mechanical Keyboard

Compact 60%, wired

Tom's Hardware's best budget 60% board. It has lubed linear switches, hot-swap sockets, per-key RGB and, unusually for this size, arrow keys. This listing is the wired version.

Good: Hot-swap; arrow keys; looks premium for the price.
Watch for: Lighting is changed with key combos, not software.

Mouse

Logitech G305 Lightspeed Wireless Mouse

Wireless mouse

RTINGS names it the best budget gaming mouse for most people. The egg-shaped shell fits nearly all hand sizes, and it connects wirelessly through Logitech's Lightspeed dongle.

Good: Comfortable for most hands; wireless; easy first pick.
Watch for: Not the lightest mouse around.

Headset

HyperX Cloud Stinger 2 Gaming Headset

Wired headset

Sold for roughly $50 and praised by RTINGS for its design and mic at the price. It has 50 mm drivers, memory-foam ear cushions, rotating earcups, on-headset volume and a swivel-to-mute mic.

Good: Comfortable; good mic for the price; easy controls.
Watch for: No wireless; basic materials; little tuning.

Microphone

JLab Go Talk USB-C Microphone

USB mic

TechRadar's pick if you are on a budget. It has two pickup patterns, a steady stand, a headphone jack for zero-delay monitoring, and mute and volume buttons.

Good: Two patterns; headphone monitoring; stable stand.
Watch for: Volume can jump at high gain; picks up vibration.

Controller

GameSir Nova Lite 2 Wireless PC Controller

Wireless controller

PC Gamer's best budget controller at about $30. You get Hall effect sticks, back buttons, trigger stops and a mechanical D-pad, wirelessly over a dongle or Bluetooth.

Good: Hall effect sticks; back buttons; wireless.
Watch for: Slippery grips; small shoulder buttons; about 8 hours of battery.

Questions

What should I buy first?

The monitor and a comfortable headset change the experience the most. The mouse is next.

Do I need all of it?

No. Buy what you lack and skip the rest. The controller and mic are optional extras.
